# Evidence-status ledger for the Glossa submission

This ledger fixes the submission-facing evidential status of every result named
in the main paper or formal supplement. It is a guard against turning proposed
tests into completed findings during compression, citation, or cover-letter
work.

| Item | Status | What may be claimed | What may not be claimed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Deterministic and stochastic adoption-retention simulations | Executed, model-internal | Under the declared parameter conditions, the model admits separated population regimes; removing those conditions removes the separation in the reported checks | Empirical validation of real speech communities or a general proof of long-run population behaviour |
| Closed-loop and likelihood release suite | Executed, model-internal | The declared implementation passes 328 checks and the reported sweep has recorded hashes | A fitted shared-latent likelihood for linguistic data |
| Bifurcation/hysteresis diagnostic | Executed, model-internal | The stipulated local normal form passes the reported cusp and deterministic hysteresis diagnostics | Evidence that a linguistic population has undergone a cusp transition |
| Variance/autocorrelation early-warning gate | Executed negative result | The finite-population gate failed in the reported simulation and is not used as a prediction | Critical slowing down as an established empirical or model result |
| Opportunity-loss simulation | Executed adverse result | Under heterogeneous priors alone, the evidence mean moves before between-speaker variance in all 36 reported runs | Dispersion generally leads categorical decline |
| Programmed repair comparison | Executed wiring check | The candidate repair channels are connected as specified | Evidence that repair maintains or controls a real convention |
| Lean development | Executed structural sanity check | The stated constraint-algebra and profile correspondences compile; the operator extension checks logical independence in a partial structural surrogate | Formalization of empirical paradigm identification, the graded operator profile, or causal attribution; verification of the statistical dynamics, empirical classifications, or linguistic theory as a whole |
| COCA independent-relative *whose* check | Executed corpus absence check | The reported query found no token; raw absence is uninformative without a defensible opportunity denominator | Validation that the construction is licensed, unlicensed, or rare at a known rate |
| Agreement/opportunity corpus probe in the formal supplement | Executed production-choice illustration | A rate per independently defined opportunity can be computed and treated as evidence about choice | Licensing from production rate alone, without independent alternatives and avoidance estimates |
| Universal Dependencies left-branch sample cited from Reynolds (2026) | Externally sourced completed result | The cited study reports no genuine discontinuities in its stated sample and measurable contiguity-preserving alternatives | A complete estimate of the missing form's counterfactual choice probability |
| Bresnan et al. dative model | Externally sourced completed result | The cited model estimates selection between two licensed dative realizations in its study domain | A grammaticality probability |
| BNC2014 dative rescore | Proposed | A frozen production model can be rescored on later British conversation to test transport and selection | An analysis already run for this paper |
| Regression-discontinuity response test | Proposed confirmatory design | A preregistered discontinuity can distinguish a decision-layer switch from a smooth read-out | An observed discontinuity or evidence about constitutive licensing from the design alone |
| Longitudinal concentration, cohort, transmission, artificial-language, L2, and repair studies | Proposed empirical discriminators | Each design states a conditional prediction and a failure condition | Completed population validation |
| Turkish borrowing-to-lexicalization pathway | Illustrative historical hypothesis | Narrow situational licensing can, in principle, broaden through repeated adoption and retention while suffix harmony remains productive | A documented longitudinal history of *doktor* established by this paper |
