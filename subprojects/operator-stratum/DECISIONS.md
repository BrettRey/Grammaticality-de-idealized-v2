## 2026-08-22

- **Define operator membership by closure and paradigm-level public update.** Opportunity count, preemption, categoricality, and resistance to exposure are consequences or modulators, not membership criteria.
- **Replace exclusive strata with intersecting dimensions.** Constructional licensing, operator membership, value selection, and exponent licensing answer different questions about the same token.
- **Classify *le hiver* and *depend of* at different loci.** The French example preserves definite reference but selects an excluded allomorph; English *depend of* violates a community-specific, head-narrowed complement construction without mis-setting an operator value.
- **Reject the grammar/style inference.** A closed, high-opportunity non-operator contrast can be categorically grammatical or ungrammatical. Public-update role predicts the narrower locus of comprehension disruption and repair, not grammaticality as a whole.
- **Keep history and dynamics at their own levels.** Recruitment is a diachronic support hypothesis, synchronic membership depends on the current paradigm, and population persistence requires adoption and retention rather than speaker-level updating alone.
- **Do not add simulation or Lean for this revision.** The revised predictions call for independent measurements of closure, opportunity, public-update contribution, and repair locus; no definition-complete formal theorem or useful new simulation follows yet.

## 2026-08-23

- **Query *Theoretical Linguistics* before making another normal-journal submission.** Brett approved a presubmission query for a target article after a focused current-debate bridge and roughly 100-word abstract are complete. The query format matches the paper's broad, contestable cross-school proposal. Record: `submission/venue-decision-2026-08-23.md`.
- **Use *Lingua* as the immediate fallback.** If the query is declined, prepare a fresh *Lingua* package with its required abstract, highlights, disclosure, and anonymization rather than reusing the *Functions of Language* files.
- **Do not wait for the main paper's desk outcome.** The operator query can follow the main paper's *Glossa* submission as soon as its own prerequisites are met; it is neither a simultaneous submission nor a competing venue for the same manuscript.
- **Adopt Nørgård-Sørensen and Heltoft's paradigm architecture as the structural scaffold, not as a wholesale definition of grammar.** For this paper, an identified paradigm has a bounded contrast set, a domain, a shared semantic frame, and obligatory value selection in that domain. Asymmetric markedness is treated as a strong empirical indicator rather than an inflexible admission rule.
- **Replace graded operator membership with three separate claims.** Paradigm identification is categorical for the comparison; the operator profile is the independently measured degree to which a paradigm conventionally configures public update; and the operator-stratum hypothesis is the held-out prediction that the wrong-value versus value-preserving-exponent repair difference increases with that frozen profile.
- **Let the central prediction fail without redescribing the category.** Both category placement and the repair codebook must be fixed before outcomes are inspected. If the profile-by-error-locus interaction disappears after the specified structural and social controls are matched or accounted for, the explanatory hypothesis fails even if the paradigm descriptions and operator profiles remain useful.
- **Supersede the earlier inferential label with the operator-profile hypothesis.** “Operator stratum” remains informal shorthand for the higher-profile region and does not introduce a thresholded membership category. The sole confirmatory claim is the cross-paradigm profile-by-error-locus interaction; the within-paradigm wrong-value versus value-preserving-exponent difference is only a manipulation check.
- **Operationalize paradigm admission before outcome collection.** The primary protocol uses bounded alternatives, a recurring domain, a shared semantic frame, and obligatory selection; it distinguishes zero realization from absence, specifies stopping and productivity rules, and records open residuals. Because markedness is constitutive for Nørgård-Sørensen and Heltoft, report a strict five-criterion sensitivity analysis rather than silently claiming full fidelity.
- **Test a general-consequence rival.** Stage 1 and Stage 2 use different tasks and items, with profile weights and missing-data rules frozen in advance. A broad independently measured interpretive-consequence covariate must be allowed to subsume the narrower profile account; if it does, the distinctive operator attribution fails.
- **Keep the manuscript live rather than replacing it with a supersession note.** The honest response to the literature audit is a cumulative rewrite of the paper. Supersession-note drafts are working records, not the public deliverable.
- **Treat Heltoft's programme as the foundation and closest predecessor.** The three-volume *Grammatik over det Danske Sprog* anticipates paradigm structure plus interactional organization extensionally in several Danish systems, while Nørgård-Sørensen and Heltoft provide the general paradigm scaffold. The paper claims neither ingredient nor their Danish conjunction as new.
- **Locate the residual contribution in reclassification and projectibility.** The operator profile reclassifies identified paradigms for one explanatory purpose; it earns its keep only if the frozen profile predicts the held-out value-versus-exponent repair-locus difference across paradigms.
- **Measure Boye and Harder's account as a rival.** Discursive secondary status and its focusability/addressability diagnostics enter Stage 1 alongside broad interpretive consequence. If either subsumes the profile interaction, the operator-specific attribution fails.
- **Represent the profile as a vector before reducing it.** Move type, commitment/source, discourse continuity, and response relevance remain visible components. The primary confirmatory scalar is their equally weighted root-mean-square after standardization, with zero effects retained rather than dropped as inapplicable.
- **Permit corpus-only implementation.** A corpus design may use disjoint profiling and held-out outcome partitions under the same frozen protocol; the theory does not require a newly recruited participant sample in every first test.

## 2026-08-24

- **Restore selective grammatical classification as the paper's explanandum.** The repair-centred framing is superseded. The paper asks why interpretable deviations are assigned to grammar rather than lexical, phonological, orthographic, stylistic, pragmatic, or social targets.
- **Use candidate form--value assembly relations as the neutral bearers.** “Grammatical system” presupposes the category, while “system” and “form--meaning pairing” overgenerate. The independently assigned grammatical-choice profile has three blocks: semiotic/constructional assembly, contrastive choice architecture, and conditioned community licensing.
- **Treat strict paradigms as anchors, not the extension of grammar.** Nørgård-Sørensen and Heltoft's full paradigm architecture identifies a strong region and supplies the admission rule for the narrower operator comparison. Construction-sized and item-restricted dependencies can occupy graded boundary regions.
- **Order the projections P1 → P2 → P3.** P1 predicts held-out grammar-directed fault attribution from the frozen grammatical-choice profile. P2 predicts variable or moving attribution from intermediate or changing profiles. P3 predicts the public-update-profile-by-value/realization repair difference within eligible paradigms. P3 is downstream and no longer the paper's sole confirmatory claim.
- **Import OVMG's decomposition without importing its dynamics.** Conditioning, coverage, compatibility, saturation, licensing, population status, evidence, and subjective read-out remain distinct. Opportunity and preemption can explain licensing concentration but do not define a grammatical target.
- **Retain *le hiver* as a medium-sensitive interface case.** It preserves definiteness, number, gender, and reference while failing ordinary article realization before an *h muet* noun. It is not concord, a wrong value, an established operator case, or a complete matched design; speech, writing, pausing, learner correction, and norm centre may shift its attribution.
- **Supersede the universal RMS profile rule.** Profile components remain visible; a concrete study preregisters ordinal or pairwise comparisons or a study-specific composite and weighting rule. The theoretical paper declares no universal scalar.
