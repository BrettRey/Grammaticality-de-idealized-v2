# Clarity and citation pass

## Editorial rule

The article should read as a fresh statement of the argument. It should not
explain the sequence of drafts, defend itself against objections that no reader
has yet raised, or make citations look like documentation appended after the
claim.

For this paper and future prose:

- use a narrative citation when an author, work, or programme is the subject of
  the sentence or supplies the contrast being discussed;
- use a parenthetical citation when the sentence states the paper's own claim
  and the source provides evidence, background, or several converging examples;
- do not move citations sentence-initial mechanically. Their grammatical and
  argumentative role determines their position.

The current Boye--Harder paragraph should therefore begin along these lines:

> \textcite{boyeharder2012} make conventional ancillarity and discursive
> secondary status constitutive of grammatical status. Closed paradigms and
> obligatoriness are incidental on their account, and focusability and
> addressability provide diagnostics. Their proposal directly competes with the
> grammatical-choice profile as an account of status.

## Abstract

Replace the current roughly 190-word abstract with this approximately 100-word
version:

> Why do speakers classify one intelligible deviation as ungrammatical but
> another as bad wording, pronunciation, style, or conduct? I argue that these
> classifications fallibly track the relation that has failed in a community's
> inventory of conventional form--meaning pairings. Candidate relations can be
> profiled independently by their constructional embedding, contrastive choice
> architecture, and community licensing. Stronger profiles should predict more
> consistent grammar-directed attribution in held-out cases, while intermediate
> or changing profiles should predict variable boundaries among grammar,
> lexicon, and style. For strict paradigms, a second hypothesis links
> public-update contribution to whether wrong values prompt update-directed
> repair and value-preserving realization errors prompt form correction.

The abstract will omit literature positioning, rival lists, methodological
defences, OVMG, and failure-condition boilerplate. Those belong in the article.

## OVMG

Remove the acronym and the repeated paper-family exposition. The argument needs
the distinctions among conditioning, coverage, compatibility, saturation,
licensing, population status, evidence, and subjective read-out, but readers do
not need to learn that these form an imported module called OVMG.

Retain at most one ordinary citation to `reynolds2026ovmg`, where the distinction
between community status, available evidence, and subjective response is first
introduced. Cut the other OVMG references, including the separate conclusion
paragraph. Remove opportunity/pre-emption prose where it serves only to connect
the two papers; retain it only where it advances this paper's classification
argument.

## Editorial residue

1. Remove the version note from the article. LingBuzz can carry revision history
   outside the paper.
2. Rewrite from the reader's present rather than the manuscript's past. Delete
   words such as *remains*, *now*, *retains*, and *still* where they merely mark
   an earlier draft.
3. Turn repeated corrective-negation passages into positive claims. Preserve
   genuine contrasts, especially value versus realization and source cause
   versus fault target.
4. State independent identification once in the architecture and once in the
   design. Cut repeated assurances that profiles, repairs, and labels are not
   definitionally circular.
5. Consolidate the three defeat conditions in the predictions section. The
   conclusion should name the risks briefly rather than restating the protocol.
6. Shorten the long Study B paragraphs and remove design commentary that answers
   obsolete reviewer concerns. Keep eligibility, outcome definitions,
   independent partitions, rivals, and the identifying interaction.

## Citation integration

Audit every citation in context. Likely narrative conversions include:

- Hofmeister et al. and Leivada--Westergaard on source ambiguity;
- Goldberg and Sag on conventional form--meaning pairings;
- Coseriu on evaluative domains;
- Heltoft and Nørgård-Sørensen on paradigms;
- Boye and Harder on grammatical status;
- Roberts in both Amele worked examples;
- Poulsen where the text explicitly adopts his caution;
- Schegloff et al., Dingemanse et al., Fox et al., and Tsiwah et al. where their
  findings are stated directly.

Parenthetical citation remains appropriate for broad evidential bundles such as
the dimensions of public update, information-theoretic definitions, expressive
substances, and a list of alternative traditions.

## Stop condition

The pass is complete when the abstract and conclusion answer the same question,
the reader can encounter the paper without knowing its revision history, OVMG is
not required vocabulary, citations occupy their natural argumentative
positions, the three claims and their failure conditions survive, and the PDF
builds cleanly.
