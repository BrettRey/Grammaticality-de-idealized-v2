# Framing and focus: portfolio division of labour

## Decision

Keep the operator paper as a separate article, but reshape it around one upstream
question:

> Does an independently fixed measure of a grammatical paradigm's public-update
> contribution predict where repair is directed when a value goes wrong, beyond
> constructional licensing, exponent choice, general interpretive consequence,
> discursive secondary status, and social sanction?

The present hypothesis and protocol are worth publishing. The present framing is
not yet optimal because the title, opening problem, accent/lexicon contrast,
typological survey, and social-technology discussion still inherit the broad
argument of LingBuzz/009706, while *Grammaticality de-idealized* (OVMG) already
states much of the paradigm/profile distinction and the repair prediction.

The right response is neither to abandon the paper nor to persist with the current
architecture. It is to give each publication a non-competing job.

## What each text should own

| Text | Explanandum and contribution it should own | What it should not also own |
|---|---|---|
| Published LingBuzz/009706 preprint | The historical, broader coordination conjecture: selective grammaticality, the operator/payload contrast, accent and lexical choice, the three-stratum picture, and the protocol-header analogy | The current paper's independently frozen comparative test |
| OVMG | A general profile of grammaticality: coverage, compatibility, saturation, licensing, population state, evidence, read-out, opportunity, preemption, transmission, and change | A second full definition and defence of operator profiles or a duplicate repair protocol |
| Current operator paper | Heltoft and Nørgård-Sørensen's paradigm architecture as the comparison scaffold; a separately measured public-update profile; rival measures; mismatch-locus distinctions; the two-stage identification and outcome protocol; the sole profile-by-error-locus interaction and its falsifier | A new theory of grammar, an explanation of grammaticality judgments generally, or another survey of why accent and open-class vocabulary differ from morphosyntax |

This makes the current paper upstream of one OVMG commitment. OVMG may use an
operator-compatibility dimension, but it should cite and defer to this paper for
paradigm admission, profile construction, and the repair-locus test. A null result
would leave OVMG's coverage, saturation, licensing, evidence, and change claims
intact while defeating its operator-specific attribution of repair.

## Reader contract

The article makes an existing family of claims about paradigmatic grammar and
public interaction independently testable. It does so by preventing repair from
both defining the proposed class and confirming it. Heltoft's work supplies the
closest theoretical foundation; Nørgård-Sørensen and Heltoft supply the
domain--frame paradigm scaffold; Boye and Harder's discursive-secondary-status
account is a measured rival; interactional repair supplies the held-out outcome.

The contribution is architectural and methodological:

1. identify paradigms without using the later repair behaviour;
2. measure their public-update profiles without using the later repair task;
3. distinguish wrong-value from value-preserving exponent mismatches;
4. freeze rival measures and the outcome codebook;
5. test whether the value-versus-exponent repair difference grows with the
   profile across paradigms; and
6. let a null result defeat the operator-specific attribution without erasing
   the underlying paradigm descriptions.

## Recommended title and abstract

Recommended title:

> **Where repair is directed: public-update profiles across grammatical paradigms**

More explicitly hypothesis-labelled alternative:

> **Grammatical paradigms and repair locus: the public-update profile hypothesis**

The current title should go. It keeps the published preprint's question alive,
makes clause structure the explanandum, and suggests that tense and agreement
already form a homogeneous response class. The revised paper treats all three as
candidate systems whose profiles and repair patterns must be measured.

Proposed abstract:

> Accounts of grammar as a resource for public interaction face an identification
> problem: repair behaviour cannot both define an interactionally important class
> and confirm it. Building on Heltoft's functional programme and Nørgård-Sørensen
> and Heltoft's domain--frame paradigm architecture, this paper proposes a
> prospective comparison. Paradigms are first identified by bounded alternatives,
> a recurring domain, a semantic frame, and obligatory value selection; each then
> receives an independently estimated public-update profile for move type,
> commitment or source, discourse continuity, and response relevance. Held-out
> outcomes compare wrong-value mismatches with value-preserving exponent
> mismatches. The hypothesis is that their difference in update-directed repair
> grows with the frozen profile, beyond interpretive consequence, discursive
> secondary status, structural licensing, opportunity, processing, schooling, and
> sanction. Failure defeats the operator-specific attribution, not the paradigm
> descriptions.

## Version relation

The current paper is a substantial revision and narrowing of LingBuzz/009706,
not an unrelated new paper. That relation should be stated on the first page. A
compact note is enough:

> An earlier version of this paper, posted as LingBuzz/009706, proposed a broader
> coordination account of grammaticality. The present article replaces its
> categorical operator-stratum taxonomy with an independently graded profile over
> identified paradigms, drops the protocol-header explanation and claims about
> grammaticality judgments in general, and isolates the held-out repair-locus
> hypothesis.

This is a version disclosure, not a retraction note. It prevents readers from
mistaking inherited examples and prose for a second publication and makes the
intellectual correction legible.

## Relation to OVMG

State the division of labour in the introduction rather than leaving readers to
infer it:

> OVMG uses operator compatibility as one component in a general profile of
> grammaticality. The present paper addresses an upstream question that the model
> cannot settle by definition: whether public-update profile contributes held-out
> information about repair once paradigm status, constructional licensing,
> exponent choice, and correction practice are distinguished. Failure would remove
> the operator-specific repair attribution while leaving OVMG's other profile
> distinctions available for separate tests.

After this paper is stable, OVMG should retain a concise dependency statement and
one-sentence prediction, then cite and defer for the profile, protocol, comparison
set, and falsifier. Coordinate that change with the instance currently editing
OVMG; do not make it concurrently from this subproject.

## Proposed structure

### 1. The inferential gap

Open with the problem that theories of interactionally organized grammar do not
yet provide an independent comparative test. Introduce the value/exponent contrast
and the single confirmatory interaction. State the earlier-version relation and
the OVMG division of labour here.

Do not open by asking why grammar selects clause structure, tense, and agreement
over accent, style, and lexical choice. That is the published preprint's question.

### 2. From paradigm architecture to a comparative profile

Put Heltoft's programme, Nørgård-Sørensen and Heltoft's admission criteria, and
Boye and Harder's rival account before the new hypothesis. Then separate:

- paradigm identification;
- graded public-update profile;
- constructional embedding;
- token value;
- exponent licensing; and
- response cause.

Retain the predecessor table and the analytic-level table. They do more work than
the surrounding repeated examples.

### 3. Identification, profiling, and held-out outcomes

Make the two-stage protocol the centre of the paper. Keep the stopping rule,
zero-value discipline, markedness sensitivity analysis, four-component vector,
frozen scalar, rival measures, covariates, outcome codebook, sole confirmatory
interaction, and failure rule.

### 4. Building a comparison set

Merge the present clausal-host, accent/lexicon, and typological sections into a
comparison-set section. Organize cases by what they contribute to the design:

- plausible high-profile paradigms: clause type, evidentiality, switch-reference;
- possible locally redundant paradigms: agreement and noun class;
- cross-substance cases: grammatical tone, intonation, nonmanual marking;
- low-profile or rival cases: polite/plain style and T/V social indexing;
- non-paradigm or non-value controls: interjections, open lexical choice,
  phonotactics, *depend of*; and
- value-preserving exponent cases: *goed* and *le hiver*.

Keep only cases that can plausibly furnish a Stage 1 contrast or Stage 2 control.

### 5. Prediction, falsifiers, and consequences

State the confirmatory interaction once. Retain local redundancy and
cross-substance realization as stress tests. Drop satiation and opportunity
corollaries from this paper; OVMG owns those predictions. End with the consequence
for OVMG and the broader theoretical debate.

## Cuts and compressions

- Replace “Selective grammaticality and public accountability” with the
  inferential-gap introduction. The present broad opener and “answer to the title
  question” belong to the earlier preprint.
- Compress “Public accountability” to the exact four profile dimensions and the
  rule that repair is evidence, not a definition.
- Keep the Heltoft/closest-predecessor discussion and give it greater structural
  priority.
- Reduce “Why accent and lexical choice are usually different” to the low-profile
  and social-index controls needed by the design.
- Convert “Typological consequences” from a survey into a candidate register for
  comparison-set construction.
- Reduce “Grammaticality judgments as a social technology” to accountability
  covariates and response-code distinctions. OVMG owns conditioning and the
  published preprint owns the general ideological discussion.
- Canonicalize the boundary cases in the tables; remove repeated walkthroughs of
  *goed*, *le hiver*, and *depend of*.
- Remove the satiation corollary and other OVMG-dependent material that does not
  help identify or test the repair interaction.
- Aim for roughly 6,500--7,500 source words rather than the current roughly 9,700.

| Inquiry, use, warrant, revision | Green | The question, prospective use, comparative warrant, and demotion rule are distinct. |
| Non-trivial projection | Green | The cross-paradigm profile-by-error-locus interaction is not true by definition and can fail. |

| World-side order | Green | The paper does not infer a causal mechanism or controller from stability or association. |

| Level and mereology | Green | Paradigm, construction, token value, exponent, speaker response, and population repertoire remain separate. |
| Scope and field relativity | Green | The projection is restricted to identified paradigms in specified repertoires, populations, and activities. |
| Prospective revision | Green | A null or sub-threshold interaction defeats the explanatory attribution without post-hoc redescription. |
| Positioning and conclusion | Yellow | The projection is sound, but OVMG already states much of it and the inherited preprint framing obscures the paper's distinct portfolio role. The rewrite and OVMG deferral should turn this green. |

The strongest world-side commitment is therefore modest: an independently
measured profile may support a stable comparative repair generalization. The
paper does not yet claim a mechanism, active stabilizer, controller, or natural
kind.

## Iteration checkpoint

- **What changed:** Heltoft's priority did not kill the paper, but comparison with
  the published preprint and OVMG shows that the current paper's residual role is
  narrower than its title and outer sections imply.
- **Assumption revised:** the repair prediction cannot by itself distinguish this
  paper while OVMG states the same prediction. This paper must own the full
  identification-and-test architecture, and OVMG must defer to it.
- **Evidence:** the present title and several section-level explananda reproduce
  the preprint's architecture; OVMG defines the same paradigm/profile distinction,
  failure condition, and repair prediction.
- **Decision:** reshape and continue. Do not abandon the test, and do not preserve
  the current broad frame out of inertia.

## Separate-paper viability gate

The paper remains independently viable if the rewrite produces a lean article
whose distinctive payload is the Heltoft-grounded comparison architecture and
frozen repair-locus protocol, and if OVMG can cite rather than duplicate that
payload. If OVMG must continue to carry the same definitions, protocol, and
falsifier in full, the cleaner portfolio decision would be to merge the remaining
material into OVMG rather than publish two overlapping theoretical papers.
