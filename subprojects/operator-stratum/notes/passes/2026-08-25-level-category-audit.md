# Level and category audit

Manuscript: `operator-stratum.tex`
Date: 2026-08-25

## Result

The manuscript now keeps norm organization, token status, semantic/pragmatic
consequence, public response, and epistemic access separate. It also distinguishes
the target and cause of a response, although they share a bearer. No unaccepted
level drift remains.

## Hits and repairs

| Location | Type | Problem | Repair |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| §2 and Figure 1 | unmarked level shift | The earlier flow presentation could make norm, token failure, consequence, and response look like stages in one causal pipeline. | Recast the diagram as different relations among explanatory targets. Only norm-to-token-status is constitutive; the other arrows mark possible causal effects. Added that *level* does not mean ontological hierarchy. |
| §3.1 | unearned normative inference | Coordinated recurrence and distribution could be read as sufficient for a correctness standard. | Added the bridge through practical reliance in producing and interpreting novel performances as instances of the same constructional type. Kept corpus distributions, elicitation, and responses on the evidential side. |
| §3 opening | representation / world-side object | A Construction Grammar inventory could be mistaken for the ontology itself. | Described it as a representational starting point and distinguished formal analyses from the relations they model. |
| §3.1 | token identity / conformity | Making conformity a condition of token identity risked making defective instances impossible. | Distinguished utterance, recoverable constructional affiliation, and relation-relative conformity. A performance can be recognizable through preserved relations while failing another. |
| §3.3 | bearer shift | Boye and Harder classify expressions and meanings; the paper classifies relations in which expressions participate. | Made the difference in bearer explicit before stating the demonstrative cross-classification. |
| §4 and Table 4 | epistemic error / token fault | Applying a norm from the wrong repertoire appeared among token-failure types. | Removed it from the token-failure table and described it as a prior analyst error about repertoire scope. |
| §5.2 | world-side effect / estimate | A discourse-consequence profile could be read as the discourse effects themselves. | Defined it as the analyst's estimate of several possible world-side effects and kept it out of grammar's constitutive basis. |
| §6.1 | ontology / response outcome | Phonological target attribution risked defeating the grammatical analysis itself. | Separated evidence against the grammatical analysis from evidence against the response prediction. |
| §7 | corpus / practice bearer | A corpus selected for corrections could be described as containing only opportunities for correction. | Recast the limitation as selection on recorded correction occasions, not on the community practice. |

## Filler-verb and mereology check

- **Represent** is now used for orthography representing expressions, profiles and
  formal analyses representing/modeling relations, and information-theoretic
  quantities representing uncertainty changes. The bearers are appropriate.
- **Explain** is assigned to theories or accounts and tied to a stated target:
  constitution, semantic/pragmatic consequence, attribution, or response cause.
- No uses of *underlies*, *drives*, or *regulates* remain as unspecified mechanisms.
- The paper no longer has formal grammars judging, corpora knowing, norms causing
  labels merely by possessing a status, or responses constituting the norms they
  identify.

## Bridge audit

| True premise | Previously tempting conclusion | Added bridge or limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Participants coordinate on a recurring relation. | The relation is therefore a norm. | Participants rely on it to produce and interpret novel instances of the same constructional type; recurrence alone is insufficient. |
| Demonstratives form a deictic-number system and enter nominal constructions. | Their relevant relations are therefore grammatical. | The stated functional criterion is participation of independently identified signs, values, and positions in recurrent constructional assembly; the passage cites the constructional facts and makes the bearer difference explicit. |

No internal map, table, or profile is offered as an explanation merely because it
is detailed.
