# Lakoff-style metaphor audit

Manuscript: `operator-stratum.tex`
Date: 2026-08-25

## Governing frames

| Source domain | Main expressions | Entailment | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| construction and assembly | assemble, construction, position, realization | expressions are built from related resources according to formation conditions | Retained as the paper's explicit functional frame. Added that assembly need not be mechanical, serial, segmental, or overt. |
| vertical levels | level, higher/lower explanatory target | levels form a hierarchy and one is more fundamental | Retained only after defining *level* as a difference in explanatory target, not ontological rank. Figure 1 now denies a single staged hierarchy. |
| containers and boundaries | boundary, inside/outside grammar, boxes | categories have sharp edges and cases simply fall inside or outside | Removed as an organizing frame. The difficult-case section now tests distinctions and relation-specific classifications; genuine indeterminacy remains possible. |
| strata, layers, and grain | layered analysis, grain of analysis | one phenomenon has a uniquely correct vertical decomposition or resolution | Replaced with multiple-relation analysis, unit of analysis, and relation-specific evidence. |
| architecture and scaffolding | norm architecture, paradigm architecture, scaffold | theories stand or collapse as buildings | Replaced with norm organization, account, framework, or representational starting point. |
| causal pipeline | downstream application, movement through levels | norm, failure, consequence, and response follow one deterministic flow | Replaced with separate application/consequence language; Figure 1 marks possible relations rather than stages. |
| law and accountability | norm, fault, correctness, licensing, judgment | a rule-governed practice supplies standards and deviations are assessable | Retained deliberately because thin practice-internal correctness is the paper's target. The prose expressly excludes moral obligation, institutional approval, majority preference, and actual sanction. |

## Steering cases repaired

- Renamed **Norm architecture** to **Norm organization** and **strict paradigm
  architecture** to **strict paradigm account**.
- Renamed **Boundary cases** to **Difficult cases as tests of the ontology**.
- Replaced **layered** and **mixed-grain** analyses with independently supported
  multiple-relation analyses and an explicit unit of analysis.
- Replaced causal-flow language in the levels discussion and figure caption. The
  status label is not itself made into a causal force.
- Replaced **defeat conditions** with **failure conditions** or level-specific
  evidence against an analysis/prediction.
- Replaced **core/centre** table language where it implied a spatial essence with
  **constitutive criterion**.

## Deliberately retained technical uses

- **Clause chain** remains the conventional switch-reference term.
- **Locus of repair** remains a defined interactional diagnostic: what a response
  attempts to settle.
- **Argument role** and **participant role** are literal relational terms, not
  theatrical metaphors organizing the argument.
- **Construction/assembly** remains central only with the explicit anti-mechanical
  qualification noted above.

No accidental mixed frame now carries a contested inference. There are no
*load-bearing* uses.
