## Verdict before the final fixes

Ox Alpha rated the manuscript a **major revision**. It judged the cumulative
positioning credible and the separation of identification, profiling, and
outcome coding unusually disciplined. Its central objection was that the
within-paradigm wrong-value versus value-preserving-exponent contrast was too
close to the stimulus and outcome definitions to carry confirmatory weight.

## Findings and disposition

| Finding | Disposition |
| --- | --- |
| The cross-paradigm profile-by-error-locus interaction has to carry the inferential weight; the within-paradigm contrast is close to a manipulation check. | **Accepted.** The manuscript now names the continuous interaction as the sole confirmatory test and labels the within-paradigm contrast a manipulation check. |
| A continuous profile leaves `stratum` ambiguous unless a threshold is fixed. | **Accepted without adding a threshold.** `Operator stratum` is now explicitly informal shorthand for the higher-profile portion and does no work in the test. The empirical claim is renamed the `operator-profile hypothesis`. |
| Closure, obligatory selection, and zero versus absence were not operationalized. | **Accepted.** Stage 1a now specifies bounded-inventory, exhaustivity, productivity, domain, distributional-zero, stopping-rule, and open-residual criteria. |
| Dropping markedness can enlarge the admitted comparison set. | **Accepted.** The departure is stated directly, and the protocol now requires a sensitivity analysis on the strict five-criterion subset. |
| Stage 1b left aggregation weights and shared-task-method variance open. | **Accepted.** The primary profile now uses frozen, equally weighted standardized indicators; task formats and items differ across stages; a broad interpretive-consequence measure is a rival covariate. |
| The manuscript retained a five/six-level count mismatch and old category language. | **Accepted and corrected.** The prose now identifies seven total levels and five synchronic table rows; `operator categorization` was replaced by paradigm identification and operator profiling. |
| New and future-dated citations needed a precision check. | **Accepted in scope.** The six newly ingested sources received independent source audits; page anchors were added for Nørgård-Sørensen and Heltoft, Boye and Harder, Muntigl and Ventola, and Fox and colleagues. The Wiltschko, Krifka, Levinson, and Weinberger records and claims were verified against publisher or institutional records. |

## Resulting position

The paper no longer asks whether a resource has graded membership in a new
operator category. It asks whether a contrast forms a paradigm, what frozen
operator profile that paradigm receives, and whether the profile predicts a
held-out repair-locus interaction that a broad interpretive-consequence measure
does not subsume.
