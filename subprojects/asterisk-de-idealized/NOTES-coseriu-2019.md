# Pointer: Coseriu (2019) on the asterisk and on correct vs well-formed
<!-- SUMMARY: Cross-project pointer; Coseriu's lectures on correctness bear directly on this paper's topic and aren't cited here · status: unadjudicated · updated: 2026-09-27 -->

Coseriu, *Competencia lingüística y criterios de corrección* (Sevilla, 2019), bib key `coseriu2019`
in the operator-stratum subproject. Two passages bear on this paper:

- p. 37: most expressions starred as ungrammatical in linguistics books are incongruent, not
  incorrect (*He puesto a cocinar el piano*); the hearer questions the speaker's sanity, not their
  Spanish.
- pp. 95–96: correctness is a property of realization in discourse, well-formedness of the language
  as a system, so a grammar can't enumerate "todas las oraciones correctas", only the well-formed.
