evidence-status ledger, the scrubbed staging-copy rule, the cross-document build fix,

2. make the cold-reader check executable, with named readers and failure criteria;
3. remember that supplements are reviewer-facing and need their own continuity check;

## Claims rejected after primary-source verification

Ox Alpha claimed that an Ethics and Consent section is mandatory even without relevant
participant research and that the word count may belong at the end of the manuscript.
The live [Glossa author guidelines](https://www.glossa-journal.org/site/author-guidelines/)
say instead that only Competing Interests is universally mandatory (with Abbreviations
mandatory for glossed examples), Ethics and Consent is applicable to relevant
human-participant research, and the word count belongs directly under the title.

Ox Alpha also read the submission checklist as authorizing an appendix embedded in the
main PDF but excluded from the count. The live author guidelines describe appendices as
separately hosted supplementary material, while the checklist's shorter wording is
ambiguous. The plan therefore retains its conservative practice: count everything
physically in the main PDF, label that as a safety count rather than a journal rule, and
move the Turkish appendix to a separately uploaded publishable supplement.

## Adjudicated outcome

**GO.** Implement the amended plan. Do not reopen the title, reader-first abstract,
mathematics-free main, or evidence-status constraints.
