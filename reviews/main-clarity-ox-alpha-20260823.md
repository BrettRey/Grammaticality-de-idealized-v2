# Architecture-first clarity review: *Grammaticality de-idealized*

## 1. Blunt overall diagnosis

The abstract is indeed clear, and the reader-model-and-dynamics material is the best-written stretch of the paper. The problem is placement and repetition, not comprehension difficulty. The paper states its architecture three times before formally stating it: once in the introduction's three premises, once in the §2 preamble (constitutive conditions + Figure 1), and once in passing at the end of each §1 subsection ("OVMG resolves this by…"). The full reader-facing account then arrives at line 869, roughly 45% in, after a ~400-line diagnostic catalogue (§2.1) that presupposes the instrument it precedes. Meanwhile, nearly every important example is analyzed at full length two to four times: *I have 25 years* (§1.3, §2.1 twice, implications-generative, CxG), *whose* (§2.1, diag-tree, implications-generative, UBA), left-branch extraction (§2.1 twice, diag-tree, implications-generative), the anomaly/confidence distinction (intro, §2.3, §2.4, reader-model, implications, conclusion). Section 2.5, "Mechanisms of grammatical change," is an orphaned legacy unit that duplicates the dynamics module's job with none of its discipline.

So the likely concern is correct but understated. Yes, the clean reader model begins too late. Worse: by the time it arrives, the reader has already seen its content twice and half its examples three times. A typical linguistics scholar will finish §2 unsure which passages were load-bearing.

## 2. Best section order

1. **Introduction** (largely as is; it already previews the model well).
2. **The impasse** (current §1, compressed to problem statements; see map below).
3. **From an utterance to a grammaticality profile** (current reader-model state-theory section, moved up to replace current §2, absorbing the §2 preamble's operator-value material and Figure 1). This includes the conditioning state, the four questions, population-vs-evidence, projection targets, status vs feeling, and the worked-examples table as its capstone.
4. **Diagnostic categories** (current §2.1, recomposed as *application* of the now-stated model): situation-indexing cases, compatibility cases, processing, licensing (*sheeps*, canonical *whose*), preempted gaps (LBE), winnerless cells, decision tree, misattribution examples.
5. **How licensing patterns change** (current dynamics section, absorbing surviving fragments of §2.5).
6. **Theoretical implications** (as is, with deduplication).
7. **Limitations**, **Conclusion**, appendix unchanged.

This preserves the substantive contract intact: nothing in the four-question architecture, the conditioning discipline, the population/evidence splits, or the conditional-bifurcation caveat changes position relative to its supporting argument. What changes is that the machine is handed over before the museum tour begins.

## 5. Three highest-risk proposed moves and loss avoidance

**Risk 1: Moving the model ahead of the catalogue.** The model's vocabulary (operator value, conditioning state, licensing concentration) is abstract, and the current order lets concrete cases accrete meaning first. *Avoidance:* the intro already anchors all seven opening examples, and the four-questions statement already glosses each question with a case (*yesterday*, *have 25 years*, *goed*). Preserve those inline glosses during the move, and keep the worked-examples table immediately after the four questions so abstraction never runs more than a page ahead of instantiation.

**Risk 2: Disbanding §2.5.** The going-to and passé-simple cases supply the historical texture that CxG/UBA-minded referees expect; deleting them outright invites "this account ignores classic change literature" reviews. *Avoidance:* relocate the examples, not the scaffold, and attach each explicitly to a named trajectory (futurate → re-licensing; passé simple → socially driven exclusion) so they become evidence for the dynamics rather than a rival catalogue beside them.

**Risk 3: Consolidating the multi-pass treatments of *whose*, LBE, and *have 25 years*.** Each pass currently performs distinct work (empirical rarity; generative contrast; frequency-vs-opportunity critique), and a naive merge could strand an argument that exists only in the third pass. *Avoidance:* before merging, inventory what each pass uniquely contributes (done in the map above); convert the non-canonical passes to explicit back-references carrying only their unique increment. If any increment resists one-sentence summary, promote it into the canonical treatment rather than leaving it behind.

## 6. Realistic compression range

The main text runs to roughly 27–29k words. Deduplication alone, without touching the contract, supports a 20–30% reduction (about 5,500–8,000 words): §2.3 (~200 words), §2.5 (~900 minus relocated fragments), preamble duplication (~500), the §1 resolutions and example reprisals (~400), the *whose*/*LBE*/*have-years* second and third passes (~1,200), §2.4's merge into the feeling section (~600), evidentiality/progressive restatements (~300), misc. trims (~1,000+). The move itself is roughly cost-neutral. Below ~30% you start paying in protected content; above ~35% you risk the empirical-grounding texture that makes the paper feel worked rather than asserted.

## 7. Strongest case against my own recommendation

The current order is defensible as deliberate induction-first rhetoric. §1 builds the impasse dialectically, §2.1 lets the phenomena generate the taxonomy, and the reader-model then arrives as a resolution the reader has been prepared to want. Reordering converts the paper from problem-driven to theory-driven, and theory-driven openings in this genre often read as assertion-before-motivation: a referee who reaches "coverage, compatibility, saturation, licensing" on page 6 rather than page 18 has had less time to feel why no simpler scheme would do. There is also a real sense in which §2.1 is not merely a catalogue but the paper's empirical warrant; a reader who doubts the framework's breadth loses that accumulation if the tour shrinks. Finally, the triple statement of the architecture may be functioning as deliberate spacing-effect pedagogy for a interdisciplinary audience rather than as accident.

My reply: the induction the concern invokes already exists, complete, in the introduction, which presents the seven examples and the three premises in under two pages. Duplicating that induction for another fifteen pages before delivering the instrument is not pedagogy; it is the retarget's residue. The strongest version of the counterargument justifies keeping §1's problem-driven arc, and my recommendation does keep it. Nothing survives of the case for stating the model zero times before line 869.
