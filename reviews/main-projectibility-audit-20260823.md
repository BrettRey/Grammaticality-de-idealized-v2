# Projectibility audit after the main/supplement split

Date: 2026-08-22 (America/Toronto)

## Overall verdict

The reader-facing paper is projectibility-first and release-worthy on this
dimension. Its strongest justified world-side commitment is a **conditional
stabilization profile**: independently identified structure, convention,
opportunity, and repair patterns can support projections about exposure,
transmission, judgment stability, and change. Maintenance and control remain
candidate explanations requiring intervention evidence.

## Residual caution

The paper may investigate maintenance or control if intervention evidence later
supports those stronger claims. Until then, `stabilizes`, `maintains`, and
`controls` should continue to mark different evidential levels. No
homeostatic-property-cluster label is earned merely from a stable profile.
