## 1. Verdict on the restructuring

The split succeeds at its primary goal: the main paper is now genuinely notation-free (its only formula-like object is a logistic curve embedded in the Figure `fig:rd-test` TikZ code), while retaining the model's linguistic content, scope conditions, and falsifiers almost completely — the framing rank-order, artificial-language-learning, L2-transfer, concentration-loss precursor, soft-alternation control, repair-type parameter, re-licensing signature, and regression-discontinuity checks are all still stated in falsifiable form in the main text, and the main paper's descriptions of the supplement's approximations (assumed-density filter, declared adoption criterion, expected-transition closure, open bounded-memory result) are accurate, not flattering. The five previously fragile interfaces all survive deliberate attack; I found no contradiction between main, supplement, and Lean, and several cross-artifact identities check out exactly (the epistemic/heterogeneity variance decomposition, the confidence-bound types, the omission-likelihood identity, the d = .495 derivation, the Wilson intervals, the 0/1,228 rule-of-three bound). The residual problems are localized and fixable: one load-bearing mechanism in the left-branch-extraction argument ("coverage weights" / pooled licensing) is invoked but never defined; the supplement's Lean-correspondence section is stale relative to the now-complete operator-stratum artifacts; the dense arm of the flagship posterior figure cannot be reproduced from its own captioned recipe; and the notation register has a handful of genuine gaps and unflagged collisions (`e_t`, `ℓ`, `d`, `c`, `r`). None of these requires putting equations back into the main paper.

## 3. Explicit verdicts

**Main-paper linguistic coherence: SOUND.** The four constitutive questions, the operator working test, the three-way speaker/population/analyst split, the two-channel read-out, and all eight falsifiers survive intact without notation. The level discipline demanded in Q3 is clean everywhere I probed: opportunity is repeatedly and explicitly denied membership status ("Opportunity density modulates… But opportunity doesn't determine operator membership," main ≈ll. 640–655; "affects the concentration of their licensing evidence, not membership," §intonational operators); mis-setting is separated from value-intact exponent exclusion (*goed*, Turkish appendix); *depend of* is treated as head-specific complement convention, not operator error; the SBCG sign/projection discipline lives in the supplement (`section3.tex` ≈ll. 88–100: "task-specific projection… additional SBCG fields remain available") with the main paper carrying the CxG-grain statement. Only F-07 (two four-question lists) and the accessibility frictions below qualify this.

**Formal supplement coherence: SOUND WITH REPAIRS.** The five fragile interfaces all check out under attack (ledger below). The one substantive hole is F-01 (undefined coverage-weight pooling underwriting the LBE grain-asymmetry answer); the rest are register and reproducibility hygiene (F-03, F-04, F-06, F-08, F-09).

**Lean structural meaningfulness: MEANINGFUL AS SCOPED.** This is not a vacuous compile-farm. The non-vacuity engineering is genuine and targets real traps: non-empty `contributions` makes vacuously-licensed empty assemblies unrepresentable (`Assembly`, `Core.lean` ≈ll. 225–265); `satisfiable_top` prevents unit-produced unsatisfiability; `exists_licensed_node_of_licensedNodes` blocks vacuous licensing; `boolConstraintAlgebra` and `uniformPosterior` witness joint satisfiability of the assumed laws. The payload theorems with manuscript-facing content are: the clash-dooms-assembly theorem (`not_def_of_unsatisfiable_constraint`), permutation invariance of `Def` (needs the monoid laws — a real proof), the single-pivotal-node reduction (which mirrors the supplement's S^θ = θ limiting statement), the moment identity, and — in the stratum files — the **four-cell independence witnesses** (operator×licensed, operator×unlicensed, non-operator×licensed, non-operator×unlicensed all inhabited), which prove exactly the combination-space non-emptiness the paper's level distinctions require, and the two bridge bundles (`nonoperator_constructional_failure_witness` = *depend of*-shaped; `value_intact_exponent_failure_bridge` = *goed*-shaped), which are faithful to the manuscript's classifications. No empirical content is smuggled: conclusions follow from explicit witnesses, not arbitrary predicates; the one place a conclusion could hide — `isSituatedOperator_withProfile` — is trivial *by design* (profile fields are definitionally absent from membership), and its triviality is the claim. Caveats: the conventionalness stub (F-05), the coarse two-bit profile (F-10), and vacuous-only saturation witnesses (F-11). The README's disclaimers (no stochastic claims, `obl`/`supplies` primitive, no linguistic-correctness certification) are accurate and match the supplement's §Lean-correspondence.

**Main–supplement–Lean correspondence: CONSISTENT; ONE STALE JOINT.** Label routing is correct in both directions (`M-` prefixed targets `sec:operator-values`, `sec:community-values`, `sec:motivations`, `sec:implications`, `sec:subjective`, `sec:diag-tree` all exist in `main.tex`; no main-paper reference dangles into supplement-only labels). Numeric and structural identities line up across artifacts (decomposition, confidence bounds, single-node reduction, d = .495, Wilson cells). The only defect is F-02: the correspondence section predates the completed extension it describes. No contradictions found.

## 5. Reader accessibility (Q2) — three highest-friction passages

1. **Winnerless-cell mechanism** — `reader-model-and-dynamics.tex` ll. 368–375, with the technical core in `formal-dynamics.tex` ≈ll. 662–695. *Genuine conceptual opacity.* The claim that avoidance-starvation alone preserves the prior, and that the low-mean/low-contrast profile "requires an additional independently motivated ingredient" (low priors, weak diffuse negative evidence, hierarchical prior, or latent cell-level state), asks the reader to hold a negative result (starvation is insufficient) plus a four-way menu of fixes. The main-paper compression drops the *reason* starvation preserves the prior (the ℓ≈0 lemma), leaving the menu looking stipulative. Repair: one clause in the main text — "because an avoidance choice is nearly uninformative about any particular candidate" — imports the lemma without notation.
2. **Repair as maintainer/controller** — `reader-model-and-dynamics.tex` ll. 330–352 plus the predictions paragraph (main §repair type). *Density, not opacity.* The graded ladder (stabilization → maintenance → control) is stated cleanly three times, but the passage stacks a triple taxonomy, a double negation ("a difference in repair target, not a prediction that non-operators escape categorical policing"), and a falsifier in ~20 lines. Slowing the double negation into two sentences would fix it.
3. **Derived obligatoriness** — `reader-model-and-dynamics.tex` ll. 359–366, anchored by `main.tex` §community-values. *Conceptual, but example-anchored.* "Obligatoriness… is the historical result of situations in which an unmarked alternative repeatedly loses" plus "must not infer obligatoriness from the analyst's present uncertainty" compresses the ontic-macro/analyst-identification distinction into two sentences; the hysteresis and threshold machinery is (correctly) invisible in the main paper, so the claim floats until the reader reaches the supplement. The English/French progressive example does most of the rescue work.

Runner-up (density): the norming protocol in `reader-model-and-dynamics.tex` ll. 214–221 and its supplement expansion — procedurally dense but conceptually clear. The reader-facing definitions of *public update*, operator contrast, coverage, compatibility, saturation, licensing, evidence concentration, population heterogeneity, and the four trajectories are all adequate as written; the friction is concentrated in the three passages above.
