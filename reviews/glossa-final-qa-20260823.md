## Outcome

The reader-facing paper now presents one central theoretical contribution: an
integrated grammaticality profile that separates population conditions,
speaker and analyst evidence, subjective read-out, and candidate causal
supports. Repair is the first and strongest operator-specific attribution test.
The dynamics are a conditional support-and-change module, and the simulations
remain model-internal existence, coherence, and failure checks.

The Elicit assessment was used as a framing and originality stress test, not as
evidence. Its useful recommendations were adopted: concede the precedents of the
individual ingredients, claim originality for the integration and dissociating
predictions, foreground repair locus and evidence concentration, and subordinate
the dynamics. A wholesale reorganization wasn't needed because the preceding
reader pass had already established the recommended hierarchy.

The latest operator companion required one further correction. Paradigm
identification is categorical for that paper's comparison; an identified
paradigm then receives a graded public-update operator profile; and the informal
operator stratum is the high-profile end. The main paper, Turkish supplement,
formal supplement, evidence ledger, and Lean scope statements now follow that
architecture. Paradigm identification, profiling, constructional licensing,
value selection, exponent licensing, and causal attribution remain separate.
