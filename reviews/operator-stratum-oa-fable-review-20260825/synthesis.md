# Synthesis: Ox Alpha and Fable reviews
**Target:** `subprojects/operator-stratum/operator-stratum.tex`
**Venue frame:** _Linguistics and Philosophy_
**Reviews:** Ox Alpha clean room; Claude Fable clean room; Ox Alpha in a Cailin O'Connor-style analytical persona
## Direct verdict
The reviews support a focused conceptual revision, not another reconstruction. All three regard the level architecture, the distinction among value-error types, the anti-circularity methodology, and the explicit defeat conditions as real contributions. They disagree about how close the paper is to submission: the clean-room Ox review returns **minor conceptual revision, bordering on major**; Fable and the O'Connor-style review return **major conceptual revision**.

The most important unresolved issue is narrower than a general theory of social normativity. The draft says that constitutive reliance supplies practice-internal conditions of correct tokening, but it does not yet specify clearly enough:

1. the thin sense of correctness being claimed, as distinct from moral obligation, sanction, or mere statistical expectation;

2. what population-level facts constitute a repertoire-relative norm at a time;

3. how the repertoire's scope is fixed independently of the disputed responses.


The paper does not need to solve the whole normativity-of-meaning debate, model language change, or make repair constitutive of grammar. It does need a more explicit answer to those three questions.
## Convergence that survives the shared prompt
The clean-room prompt supplied the artifact, venue, and review task but no prior diagnosis. Ox Alpha and Fable independently recomputed several manuscript facts:

- No case executes the paper's evidential sequence end to end. The boundary cases list the relevant evidence and defeat conditions, but none begins with a frozen assembly-role profile and carries it through to a held-out outcome.

- The normativity bridge is concentrated in a short passage in §3.1. It distinguishes regularity from correctness but does not fully state the synchronic constitution relation.

- The flagship French case remains correctly conditional, and the Amele comparison remains explicitly unvalidated. These are honest limitations rather than hidden overclaims.

- The Boye--Harder comparison identifies a possible disagreement but supplies no verified extensional divergence.


Agreement on the first point is especially informative because neither clean-room review was told to look for a missing worked profile. Agreement on normativity is less independent once the O'Connor-style review is counted, because its prompt explicitly asked about the practice-to-correctness transition.
## Disagreements
### Is the normativity bridge already successful?
Clean-room Ox calls constitutive reliance one of the paper's strongest advances, while Fable calls it underbuilt. The O'Connor-style review presses harder and argues that reliance yields coordination rather than correctness. That last inference is not compulsory: constitutive rules can supply practice-internal correctness without moral obligation or actual sanction. The draft should state this thin normative claim explicitly instead of importing repair or punishment into norm constitution.
### What is the hardest boundary?
Ox emphasizes the exclusion of pure phonotactics and residual circularity in the counterfactual test. Fable emphasizes the distinction between head-specific licensing and collocational preference, using _depend on_ and _heavy rain_. The O'Connor-style review emphasizes scope, partial sharing, and indeterminacy. These are not interchangeable objections:

- phonotactic norms can be excluded from the paper's target if the functional distinction is argued rather than stipulated;

- the licensing/collocation case tests whether that distinction can be applied;

- scope tests whether any answer can be protected by retrospective re-population.

### How much empirical work is needed now?
Both clean-room reviews ask for one executed profile, and Fable suggests a pilot. The stronger demand is unnecessary for this paper. A compact worked evidential reconstruction from existing French data would show that the procedure can be executed without turning the article back into an uncompleted participant study. The held-out predictions can remain a prospective research programme.
## Adjudication of the principal objections
### Accept
1. **Clarify thin normativity and synchronic constitution.** State that the relevant norm is a practice-internal standard of correct constructional tokening, not a moral obligation. Say what distributed production, interpretation, reciprocal expectation, and transmission facts constitute it at a time while allowing an individual response to misidentify it.

2. **Fix scope ex ante.** Give independent criteria for the scoped repertoire (interaction and exposure networks, activity, medium, period, and distributional clustering) and forbid re-scoping after the disputed judgments are seen. Where reasonable scopes yield different answers, status is scope-sensitive rather than rescued by fiat.

3. **Show one analysis end to end.** French elision is the most economical candidate. Use existing evidence to identify the relata, constructional site, conditioning, neighbouring phonological analysis, and defeater before discussing likely attribution.

4. **Explain blocking and pre-emption.** _Goed_ satisfies the productive regular pattern but loses to an item-specific realization. The manuscript should say what relation is violated and why this supports a layered analysis.

5. **State where _depend on_ stops.** Projection is evidence, not a sufficient discriminator: collocations can also project. If current evidence does not decide whether the final preposition restriction is licensing or collocation, say so directly and identify what additional distribution would decide it.

### Reject or sharply limit
1. **Do not make repair or sanction constitutive of the norm.** That would use the response outcome to establish the status it is meant to test and would collapse the paper's strongest level distinction. Repair can maintain norms historically and provide downstream evidence without becoming a token-level truth-maker.

2. **Do not add a population-dynamic model.** The paper needs a constitution and scope statement, not an equilibrium model of every maintenance mechanism.

3. **Do not attempt to solve public-language skepticism or the full rule-following/gerrymandering problem.** Delimit the public, repertoire-relative target and state its dependence base. The paper can acknowledge related individual and model-relative targets without defending all of them.

4. **Do not treat folk use of the word _grammar_ as evidence that phonotactics is constitutively grammatical.** The paper distinguishes attribution from status precisely so those can diverge.

5. **Do not use _goed_ as a decisive counterexample to Boye and Harder until their resources for grammatical realization have been checked in the source.** Both models marked their proposed divergence as requiring verification.

## Recommended next pass
1. Add a compact §3.1 passage defining thin practice-internal correctness, its synchronic dependence base, and response-independence.

2. Add an equally compact scope-fixing rule and sensitivity condition.

3. Work the French case through the individuation procedure using existing source evidence; no participant study is required.

4. Clarify pre-emption in _goed_ and make the residual uncertainty in _depend on_ explicit.

5. Define _value_ at first use and decide whether Amele can be validated cheaply; otherwise keep it visibly schematic or remove it later.


These are drafting tasks. They do not justify another manuscript-wide polish or a return to the discarded experimental architecture.
