Act as a cold reviewer of two supplementary files for a linguistics article.
The attached text contains the anonymous main manuscript followed by the formal
supplement and the Turkish vowel-harmony supplement. Assess the supplements as
reviewer-facing documents, not as archival dumps.

Report only concrete continuity defects in these categories:

1. orphaned or unintelligible references to the main paper;
2. symbols, terms, examples, or abbreviations used before definition;
3. a mismatch between a claim in the main paper and what the supplement proves,
   simulates, illustrates, or leaves open;
4. result-status inflation, especially simulation, corpus, Lean, or historical claims;
5. missing information that prevents a competent reader from locating the
   reproducibility materials or understanding the scope of the supplements.

Classify each finding as MATERIAL, MINOR, or NONE and give a supplement/page or
section locator. Do not demand that all mathematics be repeated in the main
paper. End with exactly `SUPPLEMENT GATE: PASS` if there are no material
findings, otherwise `SUPPLEMENT GATE: FAIL`.
