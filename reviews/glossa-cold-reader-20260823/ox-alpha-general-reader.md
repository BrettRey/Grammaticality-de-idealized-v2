## Argument map

The paper argues that “grammaticality” conflates four separable questions: whether an analysis exists (coverage), whether grammatically encoded operator contributions cohere (compatibility), whether contextually obligatory contrasts are supplied (saturation), and whether the community licenses the construction (licensing); subjective anomaly and confidence are separate read-outs. A grammaticality claim abbreviates such a profile. Profiles earn their standing by projection: predicting repair, exposure response, transmission, and change. A dynamics theory models how opportunity structure, competition (preemption), adoption/retention, and forgetting stabilize or shift population licensing; simulations (in a supplement) allegedly show separated population regimes arise only under jointly strong settings. Diagnostic contrasts reclassify familiar cases: *Can the have running* lacks coverage; *I’ve finished it yesterday* fails compatibility; left-branch extraction is a candidate preempted gap; *pobedit’*/​*amn’t* are winnerless cells; centre embedding is mere processing cost. Framing/satiation effects should track evidence concentration. The account positions itself between generative grammar, Construction Grammar, usage-based approaches, logicality, and relevance theory.

## Gate 1: Specialist terms before they are earned

**MINOR** — Table 1 (p. 8, §3.9) uses “putative high-opportunity preemption,” “winnerless cell,” and the untranslated Russian example *pobedit’* first-person singular before §4.5 (pp. 10, 14) introduces these trajectory types and the Russian paradigm. The table’s caption warns that labels are shorthand, but a cold reader meets the model’s most technical vocabulary at its densest point with no definition yet.

**MINOR** — “Opportunity density” (p. 4, §3.2: “Opportunity density affects that evidence”) is used before the niche/opportunity/outside-option apparatus is defined in §4.1 (p. 9). Inferable but unearned.

**MINOR** — “Satiation” appears in Figure 1 (p. 7: “repair, transmission, satiation, change”) and is never defined in the main text; the only anchor is Lu et al. (2024) as a “nearby benchmark” (p. 16, §6.1). Readers outside psycholinguistics of acceptability will not know the term.

**MINOR** — “Hard unification” (p. 17, §6.1: “Hard unification makes a commitment that a weighted-penalty model doesn’t”) leans on §5.3’s informal “failed unification” (p. 12) without ever stating what makes unification “hard” versus a weighted-penalty alternative in the main text.

Gate 1: no MATERIAL findings.

## Gate 2: Ungrounded judgments or contextual restrictions

**MINOR** — (5) *We sheared three sheeps* (p. 13, §5.5): the ungrammaticality judgment and especially the “black sheeps of the family” figurative exception are the author’s own judgments, with no citation or corpus support, unlike the parallel *whose* case which receives a documented search.

**MINOR** — The age-predication claim about *I have 25 years* (pp. 3, 13; §§2.3, 5.5), including that English “licenses have+years for duration, remaining time, or experience” but not age, is asserted without citation or corpus evidence even though the paper’s own methodology section demands independent norming for exactly this kind of restriction.

**MINOR** — Turkish evidential marking as an obligatorification case (p. 10, §4.5: “English progressive marking and Turkish evidential marking are proposed cases”) is uncited; the cross-linguistic generalization is left to reader background.

**MINOR** — Claims that the English polar-question rise and nuclear-accent placement have public-update roles (p. 18, §6.1) rest solely on self-citation (Reynolds, 2026b); a cold reader cannot assess a load-bearing operator classification from the manuscript itself.

No judgment is wildly implausible, and several risky ones (*whose*, COCA zero result, *amn’t*) are explicitly hedged. Gate 2: no MATERIAL findings.

## Gate 3: Links unevaluable without the supplement

**MATERIAL** — The simulation claims (Abstract, p. 1: “Simulations show that specified adoption conditions can generate sharply separated population patterns”; §4.3, pp. 9–10; Conclusion, p. 22: “the population dynamics establish the possibility of separated licensing regimes”). The main text reports no parameters, thresholds, regime descriptions, or outputs; the entire quantitative basis is the qualitative gloss “only when adoption response, opportunity, memory, and competition are jointly strong enough” (p. 9). Since this is one of the abstract’s three headline results and the paper’s distinctive contribution over Blythe & Croft is claimed to be the joint formalization, a reviewer reading only the manuscript cannot evaluate the premise-to-conclusion link at all.

**MINOR** — The “agreement corpus probe reported there” (p. 8, §3.8) and the “machine-checked structural correspondence” (p. 23) are invoked as illustrations/support but contain no reportable results in the main text.

**MINOR** — §6.5 (p. 20): the “restricted conditions under which an attributable omission provides approximately direct evidence of non-licensing” are deferred to the supplement; the qualitative version in §4.1 (p. 9) is given, but the epistemically crucial qualifier is not evaluable from the manuscript.

## Gate 4: Falsifiers with unclear targets

**MINOR** — §6.1, “Concentration loss before categorical decline” (p. 16): the primary falsifier (concentration-sensitive measures drop while population rate stays stable) is immediately tangled with a second, explicitly conditional claim about inter-speaker dispersion (“A dispersion-leading result should be predicted only when…”). On first read it is unclear whether a dispersion-leading outcome confirms, disconfirms, or is orthogonal to the destabilization precursor; the target needs untangling.

All other falsifiers (§3.2 operator test, p. 4; §4.5 trajectory falsifiers, pp. 10–11; §5.4 left-branch Prediction, p. 14; §6.1 rank order, re-licensing signatures, and history-dependence, pp. 16–17; decision-layer check, p. 21) state their targets and failure conditions clearly, including the admirable discipline of fixing situations before outcomes.

**GATE: FAIL**

One material finding: the simulation results underwriting the abstract’s central “sharply separated population patterns” claim are not evaluable from the main manuscript alone (§4.3, pp. 9–10). Everything else is polish-level.
