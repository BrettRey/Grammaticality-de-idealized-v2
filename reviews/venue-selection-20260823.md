## Recommendation

| _Why clause structure is judged like tense and agreement_ | Send a presubmission query to _Theoretical Linguistics_ for a target article | Submit to _Lingua_ if the query is declined | The operator paper is a broad, contestable cross-school hypothesis of exactly the kind the target-article/commentary format is designed for. I would not wait for the main paper to clear desk review before querying _Theoretical Linguistics_. These are different venues and the query does not create a simultaneous submission. I would submit the main paper first, then send the operator query as soon as the current-literature bridge is ready. If the query is declined, _Lingua_ can receive the operator paper without waiting for a _Glossa_ decision.
## What changed from the earlier venue view
- _Linguistics_ is substantively strong for the main paper, but the portfolio already has _Countability_ there. It is now a conditional fallback, not the live first choice.

- _Journal of Linguistics_ is unavailable while `LIN-2026-0100` remains live because the journal permits only one paper by the same author at a time.

- _Language Sciences_ is already occupied by _What do we mean by language?_ and is too close to the same programme to use simultaneously for the operator paper.

- _Glossa_ is free.

- The current De Gruyter Brill AI policy does **not** categorically exclude AI-assisted manuscripts. It excludes wholly or primarily AI-generated papers, requires disclosure of significant AI contributions, and leaves full responsibility with the human author.

## Submission prerequisites
### Main paper → _Glossa_
1. Cut roughly 700--1,000 words for a safe margin below the 13,000-word initial limit, which includes references and citations. `texcount` reports 10,919 source words before the rendered bibliography; PDF extraction gives about 13,500 overall.

2. Engage Christensen & Nyvad (2024) on complexity/frequency/acceptability and Franjieh et al. (2025) on the emergence, fixation, and redundancy of grammatical classification, but only where they sharpen the paper's actual argument.

3. Prepare a fully anonymized initial PDF and anonymous supplement/repository view.

4. Align the existing AI statement with OLH's submission declaration while retaining the manuscript's more informative model-and-verification detail.

### Operator paper → _Theoretical Linguistics_ query
1. Add a focused differentiation from Wiltschko's interactional-spine proposal, Krifka's JudgeP/CommitmentP/ActP architecture, and Levinson's 2026 argument that speech acts cannot be reduced to context change. The paper's distinctive claim must be explicit: public-update role plus paradigmatic closure predicts a boundary in grammatical policing; it is not a syntacticization thesis or a general theory of speech acts.

2. Prepare an approximately 100-word query abstract. The current 214-word abstract is suitable for the paper but not for the journal's query/submission format.

3. Tell the editors that a LingBuzz preprint exists and that substantial AI assistance is disclosed under the publisher policy.

4. If the editors decline, retarget to _Lingua_: cut the abstract below its strict 200-word keypoint, add 3--5 highlights, use the exact Elsevier AI declaration, and anonymize self-citations and the reference list according to its unusually strict instructions.
