# Second-Opinion Review: Formal Apparatus of *Grammaticality de-idealized* (OVMG)

**Scope and method.** I read every substantive source in the bundle: `paper/main.tex`, `paper/section3.tex`, `formalization/OVMG/Core.lean` (+ `OVMG.lean`, `lakefile.toml`, `lean-toolchain`, `formalization/README.md`), `ovmg-tools/README.md`, `ovmg-tools/quantitative-model-contract.md`, `ovmg-tools/js/revised-engine.mjs`, `revised-sim.mjs`, `joint-likelihood.mjs`, and the three context documents (`projectibility-first-wtwss-codex-brief.md`, `formal-dynamics-revision-plan-2026-07-10.md`, `DECISIONS-excerpt`). Because the rendering does not expose stable line numbers, I anchor every citation to file plus equation label, theorem name, or exported function name, each of which is uniquely locatable. I recomputed the displayed identities, checked the plotted figure coordinates against the stated generative process, verified the Wilson intervals in the agreement table, and constructed counterexamples where the interfaces looked suspect.

---

## 1. Overall verdict

**Conditional pass with required repairs.** The conceptual architecture is unusually disciplined for a linguistics manuscript: the ontic/population, speaker-state, learner-posterior, and subjective-read-out levels are genuinely separated and the separation survives into the Lean types and the JavaScript contract. The core probabilistic identities are correct — I verified the variance decomposition, the discounted-update algebra, the separatrix of the cubic normal form, the figure coordinates, and the Wilson intervals, and found no numerical errors. However, publication-level claims are not yet borne by the apparatus for three consequential reasons:

1. **The joint likelihood does not actually share the latents its documentation claims it shares** (`joint-likelihood.mjs`, `readoutLogLikelihood` / `repairLogLikelihood`): the rating, confidence, and repair channels consume exogenous per-observation inputs (`readout.statusEstimate`, `observation.misSet`) that are never derived from the speaker's inclusion vector, the lect assignment, or the model's node rates. Only the production channel is wired to the latents. This is precisely the failure mode audit item 6 targets.
2. **The executable demonstrates open-loop epistemic concentration, not the closed-loop population bimodality that §4.3 advertises.** In `revised-sim.mjs` (`makePopulation`), speaker inclusion vectors are drawn once from fixed rates and frozen; only the analyst-side evidence state evolves. The feedback loop that generates the cubic's attractors — production feeding back into population licensing — is therefore not exercisable by any test in the bundle, and the "regime tests" of the contract cannot, even in principle, exhibit emergent categoricality.
3. **The Lean scaffold proves definitional unfoldings, and one of its definitions diverges from the paper's semantics.** `Combined` folds `alg.meet` over a list with no associativity/commutativity laws on `ConstraintAlgebra`, so `Def` is order-dependent, whereas the paper's $\omega(A)=\bigsqcup_{\kappa\in A}\omega_\kappa$ is order-free (§`sec:objects`). Compilation of this scaffold establishes almost nothing about paper fidelity — which the README honestly concedes, but the temptation to cite "machine-checked" status must be resisted.

None of these undermines the state theory itself. They block the stronger *dynamic* and *inferential* claims until repaired.

## 2. Genuine strengths

These survive the defects below and should be preserved verbatim during repair:

- **Level separation is real, not rhetorical.** $z_{i,t}(\kappa,c)$ (speaker state), $\theta_t(\kappa,c)$ (ontic population rate), $S_t^\theta$ vs. $\widehat S_t$ (target vs. estimate; §`sec:posterior-existence`), and $(F,\Phi)$ (read-out) are kept distinct in the paper, mirrored by `PopulationStatus` vs. `PosteriorStatus` in `Core.lean` (the latter literally carries a `target` field), and respected by the contract's insistence that $q(c\mid\text{cue})$ be derived, not generative. The 2026-07-10 revision plan was executed, not just acknowledged.
- **The mathematics I could check is correct.** For $\theta\sim\mathrm{Beta}(a,b)$: $\mathrm{Var}=C(1-C)/(\nu+1)$ and $E[\theta(1-\theta)]=C(1-C)\nu/(\nu+1)$, summing to the predictive variance $C(1-C)$ — all verified, matching §`sec:projectible-use`. The separatrix $C^\ddagger=(\beta+\chi\psi)/(\alpha+2\chi\psi)$ of eq. (`cubic-normal-form`) is algebraically correct with the stated stability pattern. Figure `fig:posterior-means` reproduces $\mathrm{Beta}(1,1)$ updating with per-step masses 0.01 and 5 *exactly* (e.g., dense mean at step 1 is $1/7\approx0.1429$; the 95% band endpoint 0.4593 equals $1-0.025^{1/6}$), and the rare curve hits 0.4000 at step 50 as $1/2.5$ requires.
- **The discounted update preserves the prior.** `updateEvidence` discounts `positiveEvidence`/`negativeEvidence` and stores `priorA`/`priorB` separately, so a quiet node returns to $\mathrm{Beta}(a_0,b_0)$, not $\mathrm{Beta}(0,0)$ — exactly the corrected filter the revision plan demanded, and matching eq. (`beta-update`)'s surrounding prose.
- **Omission evidence is implemented as specified.** `omissionLogLikelihoodRatio` computes $\ell=\log P(y\mid Z_x{=}0)-\log P(y\mid Z_x{=}1)$ and `routeOmissionEvidence` splits $r[\ell]_+$/$r[-\ell]_+$, matching eq. (`preemption-mass`) including sign convention and the explicit exclusion of target tokens as direct positive evidence.
- **The latent-lect layer confronts the marginal-product fall head-on.** `assemblyPrevalenceFromLects` implements $\sum_\ell \pi_\ell\prod_{\kappa\in A}\theta_{\ell,\kappa}$, and `independentMarginalAssemblyPrevalence` is deliberately exposed as "the incorrect independent-marginal approximation" so tests can demonstrate identical marginals with different assembly prevalence. This converts the paper's prose caveat (§`sec:objects`) into an executable fact.
- **The identification discipline (§`sec:identification-c`) is the strongest part of the paper**: independent forced-choice norming of $\rho^\star$ before licensing data is touched; $\tau(c)$ never fit to the judgments it predicts; niche-granularity robustness checks; localization warranted only by independently specifiable partitions; repeated post-hoc reassignment counted against the framework. This directly operationalizes the anti-vacuity constraints of the Codex brief (§9 there).
- **Support grades are kept apart.** Stabilization / maintenance / corrective control are distinguished in Fig. `fig:profile-spine`, §`sec:repair-controller`, and §`sec:implications`, with the controller claim left conditional on a stated intervention signature and a clean falsifier (repair tracking social indexing rather than $\Delta$).
- **Artifact hygiene.** The legacy lab is explicitly quarantined ("Do not present the legacy lab as a visualization of the v2 model…"), and the limitations section concedes the bounded-memory stationary-distribution conjecture and the memory burden.

## 3. Ranked findings

Severity: **High** = blocks the advertised claim; **Moderate** = must be repaired before reliance; **Minor** = local fix.

**F1 (High — interface failure). The judgment, confidence, and repair channels of the joint likelihood do not consume the shared latents.**
The contract (`quantitative-model-contract.md`, "Fit-Ready Joint Likelihood") promises channels "conditionally related through shared latent inclusion states and utilities," and §`sec:measurement-C` says a fitted version composes the channels "with shared latent $\ell_i$, $\Lambda_{i,t}$, $\boldsymbol{\theta}_t$, and $c$." In `joint-likelihood.mjs`, `observationLogLikelihood` wires production through `candidateAvailabilityFromInclusion(speaker.inclusion, …)` — correct — but `readoutLogLikelihood` takes `statusEstimate` from `observation.readout`, and `repairLogLikelihood` takes `misSet` and `divergence` from the observation record. Nothing links these to `model.nodeRates`, `speaker.inclusion`, or any assembly. **Counterexample:** score a speaker whose inclusion vector makes the target candidate unavailable (`inclusion = [false]`, single node) together with an observation carrying `readout.statusEstimate = 0.99`; the evaluator assigns high rating likelihood with no internal inconsistency signal. The channels are therefore only *cosmetically* joint. Either derive the status estimate from the latents (marginalize the speaker's inclusion against the assembly structure, as the contract's own hierarchy specifies) or downgrade the documentation to "conditionally independent channels given exogenous read-out covariates."

**F2 (High — scope limitation with overclaim risk). The simulator's population is static; the closed-loop emergence claim is not executable anywhere in the bundle.**
`makePopulation` in `revised-sim.mjs` draws all inclusion vectors once; `trueRates`/`lectModel.nodeRates` never change across the 80 steps. Consequently the regime tests can show the *analyst's posterior* concentrating (epistemic concentration about a frozen population) but cannot show population-level bimodality, attractors, separatrices, actuation, or repair-as-majority-enforcement — the contents of §`sec:emergent-categoricality` and eq. (`cubic-normal-form`). The paper is candid that the stationary-distribution claim is "a formal target rather than a theorem," and the contract says the simulator "does not prove the theorem-adjacent stochastic claims." But the contract also says the simulator "can test whether a stated combination … produces a claimed qualitative regime," and the regime list ("A preempted target … becomes low mean, high concentration") invites reading these as dynamical regimes. They are filtering regimes, not population regimes. At minimum, the contract and README should state that the population is frozen by construction and that no bundled artifact exercises the feedback loop.

**F3 (Moderate — formal error, with counterexample). `Def` is order-dependent in Lean but order-free in the paper.**
`Core.lean`, `Combined`, computes `constraints.foldl alg.meet alg.top`; `ConstraintAlgebra` imposes no laws on `meet`. The paper (§`sec:objects`) defines $\omega(A)=\bigsqcup_{\kappa\in A}\omega_\kappa$ with unification as satisfiability of a join — an order-independent operation. **Counterexample:** take `Constraint = String`, `top = ""`, `meet = concatenation`, `satisfiable s ↔ s = "ok"`. The assembly with constraints `["o","k"]` has `Def` true; the same assembly with constraints listed `["k","o"]` has `Def` false. Since an assembly's constraint list is an implementation artifact, `Def` as formalized is not a property of the assembly. Repair: add associativity/commutativity/idempotence (or at least a commutative-monoid-with-top) fields to `ConstraintAlgebra` and prove `Combined` order-invariant, or fold over a quotient structure.

**F4 (Moderate — omitted model component). The credit-assignment asymmetry that carries the left-branch-extraction argument is absent from the executable.**
§`sec:update` defines stream-specific node weights: $\lambda^+_\kappa$ = parsing-posterior coverage weight, $\lambda^-_\kappa\propto|\partial\log P/\partial\theta_\kappa|$ sensitivity — the formal basis for "positive evidence pools by coverage; error/preemption mass concentrates on the most specific individuating node," which is the entire answer to the compositional-inheritance objection for *Which did you buy car?* (§`sec:update`, "Grain asymmetry"). In `revised-engine.mjs`, `updateEvidence` accepts only global scalars `lambdaPos`/`lambdaNeg`; there are no per-node weights; and the simulator's default `candidateNodeIndices` assigns each candidate its own private node, so node-sharing — the precondition of the pooling problem — never arises in any shipped test. The LBE argument is therefore a structural prose argument with no computational witness. This is legitimate (the corpus companion `reynolds2026lbe` carries the empirical load), but the paper should not imply the dynamics module implements the asymmetry, and the toolchain should eventually grow a shared-node test with asymmetric routing.

**F5 (Moderate — underived approximation). The factorization $\pi_t\approx G_t\rho_t^\star$ fails even at perfect availability because of the outside option.**
$\rho^\star$ is a softmax over $\mathcal{V}^\star_{n,t}$ alone (§`sec:usage`), while the gated production model's denominator contains $e^{U_\bot}$. **Numerical counterexample:** two candidates with utilities $[0,0]$, outside utility $0$, both available ($G_t=1$). Then $\pi(x)=1/3$ for each candidate but $G_t\rho^\star(x)=1\cdot\tfrac12=\tfrac12$. The discrepancy is not a small-mass effect; it is a normalization effect present at $G_t=1$. §`sec:update`'s derivation quietly absorbs this into $g_i$ (the ratio identity holds only with $g_i=\Sigma_{\text{cand}}/(e^{U_\bot}+\Sigma_{\text{cand}})$, which is not "producer-side expected availability"), and §`sec:usage`'s statement of the approximation mentions only the pivotal-gate and independent-norming conditions. Add the missing condition (negligible outside-option mass, or define $\rho^\star$ over the gated denominator) at the equation where the factorization is introduced.

**F6 (Moderate — formal looseness / implicit fixed point). Saturation is defined via a quantity defined by the posterior that status is defined over.**
$\operatorname{sat}(A,c)$ quantifies over $\mathrm{OBL}(c,A)$; §`sec:derived-obligatoriness` defines $\mathrm{OBL}$ by estimated licensing below tolerance $\epsilon$ with concentration above $\nu_{\min}$ — i.e., a functional of the very posteriors that enter $L_t(A,c)$ inside eq. (`Gtheta`). As written, $S_t^\theta$ is an implicitly defined object (a fixed point across windows), and no well-foundedness, existence, or uniqueness remark is made. The revision plan recommended exactly this demotion ("a derived macro used for classification, not an independent primitive"), and Table `tab:notation-bridge` says "derived," but the constitutive conjunction in eq. (`Gtheta`) presents `sat` as if evaluated independently. A one-sentence temporal staggering — $\mathrm{OBL}$ computed from the previous window's posterior snapshot — restores well-foundedness at zero cost and preserves the grammaticalization payoff.

**F7 (Minor — interface inconsistency). Two different repair links.**
The paper: $\Pr(\text{repair})=\sigma(\eta_0+\eta_1(1-G_t)r(\Delta,\iota))$ (§`sec:projectible-use`). The engine: `repairProbability` returns `baseline + (1-baseline)·gain·(1-statusEstimate)·(1-exp(-divergence·footing))` — a product form, not a logistic. Both are monotone in $(1-G)$ and $\Delta$; the README calls the engine version "a bounded simulation link." Pick one form or document the divergence explicitly; as-is, anyone calibrating to the engine is not calibrating the paper's equation.

**F8 (Minor — formal mismatch). $\Phi^{\mathrm{dec}}$ can attain 1; the Lean `Confidence` type forbids it.**
Eq. (`phi-dec`) is a maximum of two complementary probabilities; `decisionConfidence` returns exactly 1 when `threshold` is 0 or 1 (`posteriorProbabilityAtLeast` short-circuits), and numerically can reach 1 at extreme parameters. `Core.lean`'s `Confidence` requires `val < 1`. Harmless in practice, but the Lean type does not type-check the engine's output domain. Relax to `≤ 1` for decision confidence or constrain thresholds to $(0,1)$.

**F9 (Minor — overclaim, internal tension). Figure `fig:agr-projection`'s caption calls the corpus share "an estimator of licensing."**
By the paper's own central distinction (§`sec:usage`, §`sec:measurement-C`: "Production is choice among assemblies, not a direct read of licensing"), a one-sided corpus rate is compatible with licensed-but-dispreferred selection whenever both agreement variants are arguably licensed. The body text is careful; the caption is not. Reword to "consistent with licensing under the identification discipline of §`sec:identification-c`; not diagnostic in isolation."

**F10 (Minor). Residual loosenesses:** (a) under situation uncertainty the pivotal-node posterior is a Beta mixture, for which "$\nu_i$" is undefined; the modal-condition convention (§`sec:feeling-new`) is a stated patch but should be flagged as such where $\Phi^{\mathrm{ev}}$ is defined. (b) The paper's anomaly drive includes $\varepsilon_i$; `anomalySignal` omits noise — acceptable for a toy, worth a comment. (c) The contract's purpose paragraph says it separates "three things" and lists four (doc bug). (d) Ascription weighting of *positive* streams (§`sec:update`) has no counterpart in the engine, where target tokens enter with weight 1.

**F11 (Flag for later verification — not charged).** Numbers I could not check against bundled sources: the recalibration slope 0.73 (§`sec:usage-based`), the COCA zero-token claim for independent relative *whose* (§`sec:core-components`), and the Verkerk et al. counts (§`sec:macro-typological`). The LBE figures are internally consistent (0/1228 with 95% upper bound 0.24% matches the rule of three: $3/1228=0.244\%$). The Wilson intervals in Table `tab:agr-cells` all verify (e.g., 71/72 → (0.925, 0.998); 14/14 → (0.785, 1.000)), which is a genuine audit trail.

## 5. Strongest world-side commitment actually warranted

**The concentration dissociation at matched mean.** The commitment I would actually bank on is: *there exist pairs of constructions whose population licensing profiles share a low mean but differ in evidential concentration, and this difference is causally efficacious — observable as differential framing/satiation lability and differential confidence read-outs — independently of any analyst's posterior.* Why this one: (i) it is ontically committed (concentration is a property of the population evidence history, not of the judge); (ii) it has a stated direct falsifier — "if measured confidence fails to dissociate preempted gaps from clean winnerless cells such as *pobedit'* 1sg at matched mean ratings, posterior concentration has no independent empirical role" (§`sec:feeling-new`); (iii) it already has converging circumstantial support inside the bundle's own citations (the *amn't*/*pobedit'* ineffability phenomenology; the Lu et al. satiation meta-analysis as nearest analogue); and (iv) it is exactly what the executable demonstrates in its honest, open-loop way (F2): identical zero positive evidence producing concentrated-exclusion versus diffuse-starvation posteriors depending on opportunity structure. Everything stronger — bimodality as population fact, repair as controller, obligatoriness as derived macro — currently rests on the conjectured bounded-memory stationary distribution and should not be presented as warranted.

## 6. Ordered repair plan

1. **Wire the joint likelihood to its latents (F1).** Derive `statusEstimate` for each observation from the speaker's marginalized inclusion/lect state against the assembly structure, and derive `misSet` from an operator-assignment field on the model, not the observation. Alternatively, if exogenous covariates are intended, rename them and amend the contract's "shared latent" sentence. Do *not* skip this: it is the difference between a joint model and a product of independent regressions.
2. **State the static-population scope of the simulator (F2)** in `quantitative-model-contract.md` and `ovmg-tools/README.md`, and relabel the regime tests as open-loop epistemic-filtering demonstrations. Then add a minimal closed-loop variant (aggregate per-window evidence feeding back into next-window rates) so the bimodality target has *some* executable witness — even a two-line fixed-rate recursion would do.
3. **Add algebraic laws to `ConstraintAlgebra` (F3)** and prove `Combined` order-invariance; this is a few lines in Lean and removes the only outright formal error found.
4. **Fix the factorization statement (F5):** add the negligible-outside-option condition at $\pi_t\approx G_t\rho_t^\star$ in §`sec:usage`, or redefine $\rho^\star$ over the gated denominator; reconcile with the $g_i$ derivation in §`sec:update`.
5. **Stagger $\mathrm{OBL}$ temporally (F6):** one sentence defining obligatoriness off the previous window's posterior snapshot, restoring well-foundedness of eq. (`Gtheta`).
6. **Implement per-node evidence weights (F4)** as configurable $\lambda^+_\kappa,\lambda^-_\kappa$ vectors plus one shared-node regression test exhibiting the credit asymmetry; until then, soften the grain-asymmetry passage to "structural argument; not yet simulated."
7. **Unify the repair link (F7)** and adjust the Lean `Confidence` bound or threshold domain (F8).
8. **Caption repairs (F9)** and the contract's "three/four" doc bug.
9. **Tempting but harmful fixes — refuse them:** (a) *Proving* operator-specific bimodality by adding assumptions to the cubic until a theorem emerges — this would manufacture the §4.3 conclusion without any empirical anchor for the added structure; keep it a conjecture with the stated $N_t\!\cdot\!\Delta$ condition. (b) *Formalizing the Beta machinery in Lean* to make the scaffold look complete — enormous cost, and successful compilation would still prove nothing about paper fidelity (the README's honesty here is an asset; do not trade it for a badge). (c) *Enriching the simulator toward empirical fit* — this destroys its value as a transparent contract; scalability belongs in Stan/PyMC per the contract's own sequence. (d) *Promoting $\mathrm{OBL}$ to a primitive* to dissolve F6 trivially — this would forfeit the derived-obligatoriness typology payoff (English/French/Turkish predictions), which is one of the paper's best assets.
