   843	Speaker $i$ represents each construction--situation pair $(\kappa,c)$ with a
   844	Beta filter over the population licensing rate $\theta_t(\kappa,c)$
   845	(\S\ref{sec:objects}); the analyst may use the same family on a different data
   846	stream to estimate that population state. The equations below suppress $i$ and
   847	describe one such filter. With perfect retention, the update is the ordinary
   848	conjugate filter for exchangeable Bernoulli licensing observations. With memory
   849	discount $\delta_m\in(0,1]$, an evidence item at occasion $t_i$ contributes
   850	$\delta_m^{t-t_i}$ of its weight at time $t$. Setting $\delta_m=1$ recovers the
   851	fixed-parameter posterior; $\delta_m<1$ is a power-discounted dynamic filter for
   852	a drifting convention \parencite{WestHarrison1997}, which is what the
   853	destabilization result in \S\ref{sec:destabilization} turns on. With no new
   854	evidence, the evidence counts decay back toward the fixed baseline prior rather
   855	than toward the improper $\mathrm{Beta}(0,0)$ limit.

   948	\noindent\textsc{Weighted omissions.} A simple preemption rule treats absence as
   949	evidential in proportion to the full-choice counterfactual probability:
   950	$p_t^-=\sum_o\widetilde{\rho}_t^\star(x\mid n(x),c)$ over current-window niche
   951	opportunities $o$. That
   952	approximation handles ordinary competitor choices but not occasions where the
   953	niche arose and nothing in the candidate set was produced--periphrasis,
   954	avoidance, repair-in-progress. Counting those as negative evidence would make
   955	every defective cell collapse into a preempted gap. The weighting follows from
   956	inverting the normalized production model.

   958	Consider an occasion $i$ where niche $n$ arose and some $y_i\neq x$ was
   959	produced. The evidential question is how strongly that choice tells against
   960	$x$'s membership in the repertoire. The additive evidence supplied by a
   961	likelihood comparison is the log likelihood ratio
   962	\begin{equation}
   963	\ell_i(x)=
   964	\log P(y_i\mid Z_x=0,n,c)-
   965	\log P(y_i\mid Z_x=1,n,c),
   966	\label{eq:attributability}
   967	\end{equation}
   968	where $Z_x$ abbreviates availability of a licensed route for $x$. Positive
   969	$\ell_i$ is evidence against $x$. Under the normalized choice model below,
   970	holding the other gates and utilities fixed makes $\ell_i\geq0$, so omissions
   971	have no separate positive-evidence channel:
   972	\begin{equation}
   973	p_t^-(x,c) = \sum_{i\in\mathcal{I}_t:\,\text{omission}} r_i\ell_i(x).
   974	\label{eq:preemption-mass}
   975	\end{equation}
   976	where $\mathcal{I}_t$ is the set of occasions in the current update window and
   977	$[r]_+=\max\{r,0\}$. The niche-identification weight $r_i$ is the posterior
   978	probability that occasion $i$ instantiated a job $x$ would have done at all,
   979	with the intended value recoverable under $x$.

  1085	\noindent\textsc{Grain asymmetry and compositional inheritance.} The two kinds of
  1086	evidence attach at different grains. Positive evidence distributes over
  1087	activated nodes by the coverage weights: a heard token confirms every
  1088	construction that covers it. Error and preemption mass instead concentrate on
  1089	the most specific node that individuates the candidate within its niche,
  1090	because that node is what distinctively predicts the missing tokens. In
  1091	Bayesian terms, the likelihood penalty for structured non-occurrence falls on
  1092	the hypothesis that wastes probability mass on sentence types never observed;
  1093	likelihood is a quantitative measure of the weight of implicit negative
  1094	evidence \autocite{PerforsEtAl2011}.
  1095	
  1096	Compositional inheritance raises an objection. Token-level licensing is assembled from
  1097	constructional nodes, so left-branch extraction activates the well-licensed
  1098	nodes for interrogative fronting, question formation, and modifier--noun
  1099	packaging. The sum can look as though it should hand the candidate substantial
  1100	licensing.
  1101	
  1102	The coverage weights block that result. A node contributes to a token's pooled
  1103	licensing only for the properties it actually sanctions, and those parent nodes
  1104	sanction only \emph{contiguous} realizations. None of them covers the
  1105	determiner--head discontinuity that defines
  1106	\ungram{\mention{Which did you buy car?}}; producing that token requires a node
  1107	that licenses a determiner--head arc crossing the verb, and no
  1108	contiguity-preserving node in $\mathcal{H}$ generates one
  1109	\autocite{reynolds2026lbe}.
  1110	
  1111	The parents keep their high licensing, each confirmed by its own abundant
  1112	contiguous tokens, and contribute none of it to the discontinuous token through
  1113	the pooling sum. The dedicated left-branch-extraction node, the only activated
  1114	node that covers the discontinuity, carries near-gating weight for it. It absorbs
  1115	preemption mass only to the extent that the missed opportunities are attributable
  1116	to competitor choice, which is why the independent $\widetilde{\rho}_t^\star$ norming
  1117	required in \S\ref{sec:identification-c} is necessary for the assignment.
  1118	The dedicated node is required by the analysis, not gerrymandered to force the
  1119	result.
  1120

  1122	just pooled parent licensing, learners should pass through a stage of treating
  1123	left-branch extraction as live. They don't need to: the candidate is generated
  1124	by analogy, so it's on the table with non-zero prior support, but its dedicated
  1125	node has no scaffolding of its own, fronting opportunities are dense in the
  1126	input, and each one adds preemption mass.

  1144	\noindent\textsc{Open-loop learning and closed-loop dynamics.} The discrete
  1145	update~\eqref{eq:beta-update} is the primary evidence-state update. With perfect retention
  1146	($\delta_m=1$) and a fixed environment (exogenous evidence streams), it's the
  1147	ordinary decaying-gain estimator: the posterior mean follows a hyperbolic
  1148	trajectory of the form $a_0/(a_0+b_0+\bar{e}t)$, as plotted in
  1149	Figure~\ref{fig:posterior-means}, and converges without bifurcating. With
  1150	bounded memory ($\delta_m<1$), it instead behaves as the stationary filter
  1151	described in \S\ref{sec:emergent-categoricality} and
  1152	\S\ref{sec:destabilization}.

  1154	Closing the loop requires both the evidence filter and the population transition
  1155	in~\eqref{eq:inclusion-transition}: production generates later evidence,
  1156	speakers' evidence states affect adoption and retention, and the resulting
  1157	inclusion states determine the next population rate. The full composition is
  1158	high dimensional. To study its possible sign structure, I stipulate the cubic
  1159	in~\eqref{eq:cubic-normal-form} as a one-dimensional qualitative normal form;
  1160	it is not derived from the Beta filter. Its fixed-point and comparative-statics
  1161	claims are conditional on the composed transition having the represented drift

  1164	\subsection{Emergent categoricality as bimodality}
  1165	\label{sec:emergent-categoricality}
  1166	
  1167	The sense of \term{emergence} used here is weak and mechanistic.
  1168	Population-level categoricality is modelled as a possible outcome of
  1169	learner-level updating, adoption, selection, and repair; it isn't a label for
  1170	whatever remains unexplained. This
  1171	differs from Hopper's \enquote{emergent grammar}, where grammar is perpetually
  1172	provisional \autocite{hopper1987}. The model allows conditioned states
  1173	with near-absorbing attractors.
  1174	
  1175	For a focal candidate in a two-way niche, take the stipulated population normal
  1176	form
  1177	\begin{equation}
  1178	\dot{\theta}=\theta(1-\theta)\bigl[\alpha\theta-\beta+
  1179	  \chi_{\mathrm{rep}}\bar{\psi}_{\mathrm{rep}}(2\theta-1)\bigr],
  1180	\label{eq:cubic-normal-form}
  1181	\end{equation}
  1182	where $\theta$ is the exchangeable population inclusion rate, not a posterior
  1183	mean. The bar marks a frozen-flow assumption: the opportunity count,
  1184	$P(\text{mis-set})$, and repair response in~\eqref{eq:repair-flow} are held at a
  1185	reference-regime value while the local phase portrait is read. If those terms
  1186	change materially with $\theta$--as $P(\text{mis-set})$ generically will--the
  1187	fixed points must instead be obtained from the resulting
  1188	$\psi_{\mathrm{rep}}(\theta)$. The normal form also applies only
  1189	where the outside option is bounded away from domination, so candidate-side
  1190	evidence responds to $\theta$. The boundary terms represent vanishing drift at
  1191	the endpoints. The bracketed term represents an expected evidence balance:
  1192	$\alpha\theta$ is positive feedback from production, $\beta$ collects
  1193	preemption, error, face cost, and outside-option pressure, and the repair term
  1194	represents majority enforcement. When
  1195	$0<\theta^\ddagger<1$, with
  1196	\[
  1197	\theta^\ddagger=\frac{\beta+\chi_{\mathrm{rep}}\bar{\psi}_{\mathrm{rep}}}
  1198	{\alpha+2\chi_{\mathrm{rep}}\bar{\psi}_{\mathrm{rep}}},
  1199	\]
  1200	the endpoints are attracting and $\theta^\ddagger$ is the separatrix. If the
  1201	transition kernel's drift has this form, construction nodes whose evidence rates
  1202	are bounded away from zero should develop a bimodal distribution of population
  1203	rates: most cluster near the attracting regions, while low-evidence, contested,
  1204	or outside-option-dominated nodes remain diffuse. The ODE is a stipulated normal
  1205	form for that population regime; the discrete Beta update remains a speaker- or
  1206	analyst-level filter, including concentration dynamics absent
  1207	from~\eqref{eq:cubic-normal-form}.
  1208	
  1209	The convergence claim has to match both levels of the model. With perfect
  1210	retention ($\delta_m=1$), an individual evidence filter is close to
  1211	reinforcement processes of Pólya type, where stochastic approximation supplies
  1212	relevant techniques \parencite{pemantle2007}. The signaling-game case,
  1213	structurally the closest analogue, is treated directly by Argiento, Pemantle,
  1214	Skyrms, and Volkov \parencite{argiento2009}, with an extension by Hu, Skyrms,
  1215	and Tarrès \parencite{huSkyrmsTarres2011}. Those results do not establish
  1216	convergence of the composed population transition
  1217	in~\eqref{eq:inclusion-transition}; that requires a specified $h_\kappa$.
  1218	
  1219	With bounded memory ($\delta_m<1$), the learner filters have constant gain, so
  1220	the decreasing-gain analogues no longer prove almost-sure convergence even at
  1221	that level. The population-level formal target is a stationary distribution for
  1222	the composed transition concentrated near the normal form's attracting regions,
  1223	with escape times and residual dispersion set by opportunity, persistence, and
  1224	memory: high-$N$ contrasts should sit tightly near the extremes, while low-$N$
  1225	or outside-option-dominated contrasts remain diffuse. That claim is a proof
  1226	obligation rather than a theorem here.
  1227	
  1228	The operator-specific prediction also needs its scope stated.
  1229	Equation~\eqref{eq:cubic-normal-form} can be bistable even when the repair term is zero
  1230	if the baseline feedback parameters $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are strong enough.
  1231	Therefore the operator/style boundary is not derived from the cubic alone. The
  1232	OVMG claim is conditional: selection intensity must scale with independently
  1233	estimated opportunity and update divergence, so that $\alpha$, $\beta$, or the
  1234	repair contribution are weak for low-$\Delta$ contrasts and strong for
  1235	contrasts with high $N_t(n,c)P(\text{mis-set})r(\Delta,\iota)$. Under that condition, dense,
  1236	update-configuring contrasts concentrate fastest, while low-$\Delta$ contrasts
  1237	remain more gradient and are policed like style. If a closed, dense,
  1238	non-configuring contrast shows the same bimodal licensing and repair profile,
  1239	the operator-specific part of the account fails.
  1240	
  1241	Bounded memory adds a quantitative residual: even near an attractor, dispersion
  1242	should scale with opportunity and forgetting, roughly with the effective
  1243	concentration supplied by the discounted evidence filter.
  1244	
  1245	Sampling drift near the extremes makes the boundaries sticky in finite
  1248	and actuation (\S\ref{sec:actuation}): a state can persist for long periods and
  1249	then move quickly once the evidence balance or outside-option utility changes.
  1250	This connects the dynamics to complex-adaptive and cultural-evolutionary
  1251	accounts of language change \autocite{beckner2009,kirby2014,BlytheCroft2012},
  1252	and to invisible-hand explanations, where macro-level order emerges as the
  1253	unintended consequence of micro-level choices \autocite{keller1994}.

  1255	\subsection{Derived obligatoriness}
  1256	\label{sec:derived-obligatoriness}
  1257	
  1258	The state theory defines saturation relative to the obligatory dimensions
  1259	$\mathrm{OBL}_t(n,c,A)$ (\S\ref{sec:objects}), where $n=n(A,c)$ is the
  1260	registered at-issue niche. Those dimensions aren't stipulated per language,
  1261	but neither are they defined by an analyst's estimate. Let
  1262	$\mathcal{A}_0(d,n,\phi,c)$ be the finite, independently fixed class of defined
  1263	assemblies of frame type $\phi$ that realize niche $n$ while leaving $d$ unset.
  1264	For $A$ in that class, define the population's \term{raw zero-marking rate}
  1265	\[
  1266	R_s^0(A,n,c)=P_j\!\left(\text{speaker $j$ realizes a $d$-at-issue opportunity
  1267	in $(n,c)$ with $A$ at $s$}\right).
  1268	\]
  1269	This is a realized population statistic, not the licensing predicate $L_s$ and
  1270	not a status estimate. The same simple-present nodes can have high licensing in
  1271	a habitual niche while their zero-marked realization rate is near zero in an
  1272	ongoing-at-issue niche. The definition thus neither mistakes general node
  1273	availability for niche-specific zero marking nor builds saturation into its own
  1274	input.
  1275	
  1276	Let $W_t=[t-W,t)$ be a preceding convention-individuating window, and let
  1277	$\Pi_{W_t}(A,d,n,c)$ be the realized pressure against $A$ there: $d$-at-issue
  1278	opportunities in which a marked competitor wins, weighted by the independently
  1279	normed full-choice share $\widetilde\rho^\star$. This weight is ungated; it
  1280	doesn't use $\operatorname{sat}_s$ or $\mathrm{OBL}_s$. Outside-option choices
  1281	don't contribute. Write $E_t(d,n,\phi,c)$ for the entry condition that, for
  1282	every $A\in\mathcal A_0(d,n,\phi,c)$,
  1283	\[
  1284	\sup_{s\in W_t}R_s^0(A,n,c)<\epsilon_0
  1285	\qquad\text{and}\qquad
  1286	\Pi_{W_t}(A,d,n,c)\geq\pi_0.
  1287	\]
  1288	Write $X_t(d,n,\phi,c)$ for the release condition that, for some such $A$,
  1289	\[
  1290	\inf_{s\in W_t}R_s^0(A,n,c)>\epsilon_1
  1291	\qquad\text{or}\qquad
  1292	\Pi_{W_t}(A,d,n,c)\leq\pi_1,
  1293	\]
  1294	where $\epsilon_1>\epsilon_0$ and $\pi_1<\pi_0$. Given an initial convention
  1295	state $\mathrm{OBL}_{t_0}$ fixed independently of the target interval, the
  1296	path-dependent macro is
  1297	\[
  1298	d\in\mathrm{OBL}_t(n,c,\phi)
  1299	\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
  1300	E_t(d,n,\phi,c)\ \lor\
  1301	\bigl[d\in\mathrm{OBL}_{t-1}(n,c,\phi)\land
  1302	\neg X_t(d,n,\phi,c)\bigr].
  1303	\]
  1304	
  1305	The entry clauses say that every zero-marking realization has persistently
  1306	disappeared under dense, attributable competition rather than because the niche
  1307	was starved or an outside option absorbed a winnerless cell. The release clauses
  1308	require sustained recovery or a clear pressure lapse. The two pairs of
  1309	thresholds leave a hysteresis band in which the preceding state persists; they
  1310	don't leave membership undefined. The timescale and thresholds are
  1311	community-indexed model parameters fixed independently of the contrast being
  1312	classified. The initial condition and strict temporal descent make the
  1313	recursion well founded: realized outputs and ungated pressure in $W_t$, together
  1314	with $\mathrm{OBL}_{t-1}$, determine $\mathrm{OBL}_t$;
  1315	$\mathrm{OBL}_t$ determines $\operatorname{sat}_t$; and saturation then enters
  1316	$S_t^\theta$. Status is consequently a functional of the recent population
  1317	history, not just of instantaneous $\boldsymbol{\theta}_t$.
  1318	
  1319	Posterior concentration belongs only to identification. An analyst counts $d$
  1320	as identified as obligatory when the posterior upper bound for every relevant
  1321	$R_s^0(A,n,c)$ lies below $\epsilon_0$, the estimated pressure clears $\pi_0$, and the
  1322	effective evidence meets a preregistered $\nu_{\min}$. Those conditions warrant
  1323	an inference about $\mathrm{OBL}_t$; they don't constitute it. Identification of
  1324	release analogously requires posterior evidence for $X_t$, not an isolated
  1325	innovation or a point estimate inside either hysteresis band.
  1326	
  1327	The proposed mechanism is the population transition plus preemption applied to
  1328	zero-marking. In a $d$-at-issue niche, marked and unmarked assemblies compete;
  1329	where the marked assembly repeatedly wins, speakers' filters and later inclusion
  1330	states can drive the raw zero-marking rate down. The normal form shows one
  1331	possible phase portrait for that process, while the stationary-distribution
  1332	claim remains a proof obligation rather than part of the definition of
  1333	obligatoriness.
  1334	
  1335	The typological payoff is that \S\ref{sec:community-values}'s descriptions
  1336	convert to predictions. On the proposed diagnosis, English progressive marking
  1337	is obligatory because simple-present assemblies in ongoing-at-issue niches have
  1338	persistently low raw zero-marking rates under a winning marked competitor; French
  1339	zero-marking there hasn't, so the dimension stays optional, and
  1340	\mention{Elle étudie maintenant} is fine. Turkish evidential marking is the
  1341	same kind of hypothesis on a different dimension. Grammaticalization's
  1342	obligatorification receives a candidate mechanism: a contrast on its way
  1343	to obligatory status is one whose zero-marking competitor is losing dense
  1344	niches one at a time, which predicts an intermediate stage--obligatory in some
  1345	frame types and niches, optional in others--that grammaticalization corpora are
  1346	positioned to test.

  1796	OVMG preserves CxG's systematic treatment of constructions while adding a prediction about which constructional contrasts attract categorical policing.   1798	\subsection{Relationship to usage-based approaches}\label{sec:usage-based}
  1799	
  1800	OVMG shares with usage-based approaches (UBA) \autocite{bybee2006, bybee2007frequency, bybee2010} the idea that linguistic knowledge develops from patterns of actual language use rather than from an autonomous formal system. Both perspectives reject the notion that grammaticality can be reduced to abstract rules operating independently of meaning and context.
  1801	
  1802	Several pieces of the formal core come directly from this tradition. The
  1803	speaker/population split restates \posscite{schmid2020} separation of
  1804	\term{entrenchment}, the cognitive reorganization of a speaker's knowledge, from
  1805	\term{conventionalization}, the social establishment of a community's
  1806	regularities. Here $z_{j,t}(\kappa,c)$ gives speaker-level inclusion states, and
  1807	$\theta_t(\kappa,c)$ gives the population licensing rate; a speaker or analyst
  1808	tracks it with a distinct posterior mean. The proposed population transition is motivated by
  1809	\posscite{croft2000} utterance-selection theory, in which variants are
  1810	replicated and selected within a community; the S-curve pattern the normal form represents
  1811	are already worked out in that lineage \autocite{BlytheCroft2012}.
  1812	
  1813	Frequency per opportunity is likewise not new: it's the core of
  1814	\posscite{Stefanowitsch_2006_NegativeEvidence} case that corpora contain
  1815	negative evidence, where a form counts as \enquote{significantly absent} rather
  1816	than \enquote{accidentally absent} only once its observed frequency is weighed
  1817	against the frequency expected given its opportunities. Variationist
  1818	sociolinguistics has normalized by opportunity since Labov's principle of
  1819	accountability \autocite{labov1972}.
  1820	
  1821	OVMG adds their joint formalization and a prediction. The
  1822	licensing-versus-selection factorization
  1823	$\pi_t\approx S_t^\theta\cdot\widetilde{\rho}_t^\star$--or
  1824	$\theta_t(\kappa,c)\cdot\widetilde{\rho}_t^\star$ in single-pivotal-node cases--turns Croft's
  1825	replication-versus-selection into an estimable product. The gated preemption mass
  1826	$p_t^-=\sum_{i\in\mathcal I_t} r_i\ell_i$ reduces to
  1827	$N_t\cdot\widetilde{\rho}_t^\star$ as a first-order approximation when the
  1828	target is nearly always omitted, niche identification is nearly certain, the
  1829	outside option is negligible, the other gates are stable, and the target's full
  1830	choice mass is small in the competitor regime, putting a
  1831	quantity on the absence that \citeauthor{Stefanowitsch_2006_NegativeEvidence}
  1832	left open, since he showed how to establish that a form is significantly absent
  1833	but stressed that significant absence \enquote{does not, in itself, provide any
  1834	clues as to why}. Licensing-versus-selection is exactly that missing clue: a
  1835	significantly absent form may be unlicensed ($S_t^\theta\approx0$, or pivotal
  1948	with practice while production and repair indicators remain stable, the effect
  1949	belongs to the implementation-cost channel rather than to the licensing
  1950	posterior.
  1951	
  1952	\item \emph{Sociolinguistic fieldwork:} Investigations in communities with distinct dialects or multilingual practices can clarify how social and pragmatic motivations shape grammatical stability. Elicitation tasks and careful comparisons across speech communities can confirm that what counts as grammatical depends on local norms rather than universal principles.
  1953	
  1954	\item \emph{Longitudinal and historical studies:} Diachronic corpora and historical grammars can trace how marginal constructions evolve, testing predictions about which factors lead certain patterns to stabilize, which fade, and which undergo reanalysis. Tracking a community's licensing of previously dubious constructions over time provides direct evidence for the gradual dynamics posited by the framework.
  1955	
  1956	\item \emph{A decision-layer discontinuity check:} The threshold $\tau(c)$ is a
  1957	read-out parameter, not a constitutive boundary. A discontinuity in response type
  1958	near $\tau(c)$ would test the decision layer: whether asymmetric losses shift
  1959	hearers from acceptance to repair, request for clarification, or sanction,
  1960	over and above the smooth effect of their own $g_{i,t}$. The general design is a
  1961	regression-discontinuity problem \autocite{LeeLemieux2010,CalonicoCattaneoTitiunik2014};
  1962	multinomial and covariate-adjusted versions are the needed extension
  1963	\autocite{Lazzaro2026}. In a confirmatory test, $\tau(c)$ has to be estimated
  1964	and registered before the outcome data is touched, and the bimodality model
  1965	(\S\ref{sec:emergent-categoricality}) predicts thin support near the threshold.
  1966	Smoothness through $\tau(c)$ would weaken the proposed decision-layer switch,
  1967	not the licensing-state theory itself.
  1968	\end{enumerate}

    96	satisfiable in the typed constraint system; otherwise the join is undefined
    97	($\bot$). Compatibility remains Boolean, but equality of atomic values is not

    99	
   100	An \term{assembly} $A$ is a finite tree of constructions with all slots filled. Its yield is $\mathrm{form}(A)$; its operator assignment is $\omega(A)=\bigsqcup_{\kappa\in A}\omega_\kappa$, defined iff all contributions unify; its value is $\mathrm{val}(A)$. Three predicates over assemblies do the core work:
   101	
   102	\begin{align}
   103	\operatorname{def}(A) &= \mathbf{1}\bigl[\omega(A)\ \text{is defined}\bigr]
   104	  && \text{(compatibility)} \\
   105	\operatorname{sat}_t(A,c) &= \mathbf{1}\bigl[\omega(A)\ \text{is total on}\
   106	  \mathrm{OBL}_t(n(A,c),c,A)\bigr]
   107	  && \text{(saturation)} \\
   108	L_t(A,c) &= \textstyle\bigwedge_{\kappa\in A} Z_t(\kappa,c)
   109	  && \text{(licensing)}
   110	\end{align}
   111	
   112	Compatibility is hard: there's no weighting an operator clash away. Saturation
   113	refers to the obligatory dimensions $\mathrm{OBL}_t(n(A,c),c,A)$, which aren't
   114	stipulated per language. Section~\ref{sec:derived-obligatoriness} derives them
   115	from a preceding window of population-level, niche-specific zero-marking rates
   116	and ungated preemption pressure, without using the analyst's posterior or
   117	saturation itself.
   118	Thus $\operatorname{sat}_t$ is a path-dependent ontic macro rather than an
   119	independent primitive or an epistemic estimate. For an assembly $A$ of frame
   120	type $\varphi(A)$ and registered at-issue niche $n(A,c)$,
   121	$\mathrm{OBL}_t(n(A,c),c,A)$ abbreviates
   122	$\mathrm{OBL}_t(n(A,c),c,\varphi(A))$. English progressive marking and Turkish
   123	evidential marking are the worked cases there.
   124	
   125	Licensing is latent, and the Beta is typed over a rate rather than a fixed binary
   126	fact. Let $z_{j,t}(\kappa,c)\in\{0,1\}$ be speaker $j$'s local inclusion state
   127	for construction $\kappa$ in $c$. The population state
   128	$\theta_t(\kappa,c)\in[0,1]$ is the latent exchangeable licensing-rate
   129	parameter. For a randomly sampled exchangeable speaker,
   130	$Z_t(\kappa,c)\mid\theta_t(\kappa,c)\sim
   131	\mathrm{Bernoulli}(\theta_t(\kappa,c))$ is the predictive inclusion state for
   132	that speaker, and $L_t(A,c)$ is the event that the sampled speaker licenses all
   133	nodes in $A$. The definition needs a joint predictive distribution
   134	over the vector $\{Z_t(\kappa,c):\kappa\in A\}$. Posterior dependence among
   135	node rates represents epistemic co-uncertainty; it does not by itself generate
   136	co-licensing within a speaker. A tractable joint model introduces a latent lect
   137	$\ell_i\in\{1,\ldots,H\}$ for speaker $i$:
   138	\[
   139	\ell_i\sim\mathrm{Categorical}(\boldsymbol{\pi}),\qquad
   140	z_{i,t}(\kappa,c)\mid\ell_i=\ell\sim
   141	\mathrm{Bernoulli}\!\left(\theta_{\ell,t}(\kappa,c)\right).
   142	\]
   143	Conditional independence across nodes within a lect is still an approximation;
   144	an explicit within-lect joint can replace it where constructional kin cluster
   145	more tightly. Under this tractable version,
   146	\[
   147	P\!\left(L_t(A,c)=1\mid\boldsymbol{\pi},\boldsymbol{\theta}_t\right)=
   148	\sum_{\ell=1}^{H}\pi_\ell\prod_{\kappa\in A}
   149	\theta_{\ell,t}(\kappa,c),
   150	\]
   151	and the marginal rate used elsewhere is
   152	$\theta_t(\kappa,c)=\sum_\ell\pi_\ell\theta_{\ell,t}(\kappa,c)$. The
   153	independent product of marginal node rates is therefore a single-lect limiting
   154	case, not a general account of assembly prevalence.
   155	
   156	No speaker or analyst observes $\theta$ directly. Under exchangeability, de Finetti's
   157	representation theorem supplies a mixing measure over $\theta$
   158	\parencite{deFinetti1937}. The Beta family is the conjugate specialization
   159	that adds linear predictive updating: in the Bernoulli case, linearity of the
   160	posterior expectation of success in the counts is the binary analogue of
   161	Johnson's sufficientness postulate in Zabell's generalization
   162	\parencite{zabell1982}. A speaker's filter has mean
   163	$C^{(i)}_t(\kappa,c)$ and concentration $\nu^{(i)}_t(\kappa,c)$; an analyst can
   164	use the same family with a different evidence set, producing
   165	$\widehat C_t$ and $\widehat\nu_t$. The linear-in-counts update rule and the Beta
   166	prior family are one modelling assumption rather than two. The population
   167	transition in~\eqref{eq:inclusion-transition} states how speakers' filters can
   168	alter later inclusion; updating an analyst posterior doesn't alter the
   169	population. The vector $\Lambda_{i,t}$ collects speaker $i$'s inclusion states
   170	$z_{i,t}(\kappa,c)$, and the population distribution over those vectors supplies
   171	the population-level status state.
   172	
   173	Compositional inheritance matters here. A novel token such as \mention{She
   174	texted him the address} needn't have been encountered: the assemblies covering it
   175	are built from nodes--ditransitive transfer, pronominal object order, the relevant
   176	lexical-category relations--each with its own posterior, and the token inherits its
   177	status through them. What the learner updates is constructional regions, not
   178	memorized sentence types, so first-heard sentences aren't predicted to have zero
   179	licensing. Actual statistical pooling would require hierarchical hyperpriors
   180	over construction families, lexemes, and situations; this core needs only
   181	the compositional inheritance just described.

   183	\subsection{Grammatical status as a conditioned state property}
   184	\label{sec:objective-G}

   186	\subsubsection{Status and its posterior estimate}
   187	\label{sec:posterior-existence}
   188	
   189	For a form $f$, value $v$, and conditioning state $c$, let $\mathcal{A}(f,v)$ be
   190	the set of assemblies with yield $f$ and value $v$, and let
   191	$\mathcal Y_{W_t}$ collect the preceding window's population realization rates,
   192	ungated pressure statistics, and prior obligatoriness state. The population
   193	state is
   194	
   195	\begin{equation}
   196	S_t^{\theta}(f,v,c)=
   197	P\Bigl(\,\exists A\in\mathcal{A}(f,v):\;
   198	\operatorname{def}(A)\wedge\operatorname{sat}_t(A,c)\wedge L_t(A,c)
   199	\,\Bigm|\,\boldsymbol{\theta}_t,\mathcal Y_{W_t},c\Bigr),
   200	\label{eq:Gtheta}
   201	\end{equation}
   202	
   203	\noindent the prevalence, among exchangeable speakers in $c$, of at least one
   204	defined, saturated, licensed route to the form--value pair. The history argument
   205	is needed because $\mathrm{OBL}_t$, hence $\operatorname{sat}_t$, is determined
   206	by the preceding population window and prior convention state rather than by an
   207	instantaneous estimate.
   208	Neither a learner nor an analyst observes this population state directly. For
   209	an epistemic bearer $r$ (a speaker $i$ or an analyst $a$), the estimate is
   210	
   211	\begin{equation}
   212	\widehat{S}^{(r)}_t(f,v,c)=
   213	\mathbb{E}_{p_r(\boldsymbol{\theta}_t,\mathcal Y_{W_t}\mid\mathcal{D}_{r,t})}
   214	\!\left[S_t^{\theta}(f,v,c)\right],
   215	\label{eq:Gt}
   216	\end{equation}
   217	
   218	\noindent where $\mathcal{D}_{r,t}$ is that bearer's evidence to date: tokens,
   219	structured omissions, repairs, avoidance, and situation cues. Status is the
   220	population property $S_t^{\theta}$; $\widehat{S}^{(r)}_t$ is a particular
   221	bearer's estimate of it. For compactness, later predictive equations write
   222	$G_t$ for the analyst's $\widehat{S}^{(a)}_t$ and $g_{i,t}$ for speaker $i$'s
   223	$\widehat{S}^{(i)}_t$. Where
   224	value is recoverable from context, I write $G_t(u,c)$ and $S_t^{\theta}(u,c)$
   225	for readability. For a single pivotal node,
   295	transparent licensing failures with low update divergence should attract only
   296	baseline understanding-repair and, if stable, be supported mainly by preemption, while
   297	operator mis-settings draw the $\Delta$-scaled repair flow; correction rates
   298	should instead track ascription and prescriptive dissonance.
   299	
   300	Other generalizations run over epistemic concentration, and they're the
   301	diagnostic ones. Two form--value pairs can share a low mean while differing in
   302	how much evidence backs it, and the update dynamics make them behave differently
   303	under new exposure: a diffuse posterior moves with framing and familiarization;
   304	a concentrated one doesn't. This epistemic dispersion is
   305	$\operatorname{Var}(S_t^{\theta}\mid\mathcal{D}_{r,t})$ for epistemic bearer
   306	$r$. Behavioral predictions use a speaker's $r=i$ posterior; analyst-facing
   307	estimates use $r=a$. In single-node cases the corresponding concentration is
   308	the pivotal node's $\nu^{(r)}$.
   309	
   310	Population heterogeneity is different. A community can have a sharply estimated
   311	interior $\theta$ because speakers are genuinely split, as with \mention{might
   312	could} in a mixed community. That state has low epistemic variance but high
   313	between-speaker disagreement. The interior estimate counts as genuine
   314	heterogeneity only when no independently specifiable refinement of $c$
   315	(\S\ref{sec:identification-c}) splits the population toward the attractors; if
   316	such a partition exists, the apparent heterogeneity was a mis-specified
   317	conditioning state. Within-speaker test--retest instability and
   318	framing lability identify epistemic uncertainty; between-speaker disagreement
   319	with within-speaker stability identifies heterogeneity. Satiation and framing
   320	rank order turn on $1/\nu$, while
   321	moribund-contrast dispersion is measured between speakers because bounded memory
   322	lets individual histories diverge.
   323	
   324	For a single node and epistemic bearer $r$, with
   325	$\theta\mid\mathcal{D}_{r,t}\sim\mathrm{Beta}(a^{(r)},b^{(r)})$, let
   326	$C^{(r)}=E[\theta\mid\mathcal{D}_{r,t}]$ and
   327	$\nu^{(r)}=a^{(r)}+b^{(r)}$. The two dispersions decompose as
   328	\begin{align}
   329	U^{(r)}_{\mathrm{epi}} &= \operatorname{Var}(\theta\mid\mathcal{D}_{r,t})
   330	  =\frac{C^{(r)}(1-C^{(r)})}{\nu^{(r)}+1},\\
   331	U^{(r)}_{\mathrm{het}} &= E[\theta(1-\theta)\mid\mathcal{D}_{r,t}]
   332	  =\frac{C^{(r)}(1-C^{(r)})\nu^{(r)}}{\nu^{(r)}+1}.
   333	\end{align}
   334	Their sum is the predictive variance of a newly sampled speaker's inclusion
   335	state, $C^{(r)}(1-C^{(r)})$. For assemblies the corresponding quantities are
   336	$U^{(r)}_{\mathrm{epi}}=\operatorname{Var}(S_t^{\theta}\mid\mathcal{D}_{r,t})$
   337	and
   338	$U^{(r)}_{\mathrm{het}}=E[S_t^{\theta}(1-S_t^{\theta})\mid\mathcal{D}_{r,t}]$.
   339	A
   340	preempted gap has low mean, low epistemic uncertainty, and low heterogeneity; a
   341	sparse form has appreciable epistemic uncertainty; a stably divided community
   342	has low epistemic uncertainty but high heterogeneity.
   343	
   344	The status regions are:
   345	
   346	\begin{itemize}
   347	\item \textbf{licensed}: $\widehat S^{(r)}_t\approx1$,
   348	  $\operatorname{Var}(S_t^{\theta}\mid\mathcal{D}_{r,t})\approx0$;
   349	\item \textbf{excluded}: $\widehat S^{(r)}_t\approx0$,
   350	  $\operatorname{Var}(S_t^{\theta}\mid\mathcal{D}_{r,t})\approx0$--confident
   351	  rejection, whether by structural failure or by concentrated non-licensing;
   352	\item \textbf{unsettled}: $\operatorname{Var}(S_t^{\theta}\mid\mathcal{D}_{r,t})$
   353	  high, in two subtypes--\emph{starved} (low concentration from a sparse
   354	  opportunity set, as with independent relative \mention{whose}) and
   355	  \emph{avoidance-divided} (low concentration despite dense opportunities, from
   356	  conflicting or absorbed evidence flows, as with defective cells,
   357	  \S\ref{sec:winnerless-cells});
   358	\item \textbf{heterogeneous}: posterior concentration high but the estimated
   359	  population rate is interior--stable within speakers, divided across speakers.
   360	\end{itemize}

   364	dimension they turn on. Left-branch extraction and \mention{whose} approach

   410	\subsubsection{Categorical talk as posterior concentration}
   411	\label{sec:categorical-talk}
   412	
   413	Communities often treat grammaticality as a membership fact: a relationship
   414	counts as an available resource in $c$ or it doesn't. On this account, a
   415	speaker's categorical talk is expected where that speaker's posterior has
   416	concentrated--where the estimated licensing state sits near an attractor and
   417	$\operatorname{Var}(S_t^{\theta}\mid\mathcal{D}_{i,t})\approx0$. Categoricality is a
   418	property of the state, not a threshold imposed on it;
   419	\S\ref{sec:emergent-categoricality} gives the dynamics under which bimodality
   420	is expected for closed, high-opportunity, update-configuring contrasts. In the
   421	contested middle, categorical talk is predicted to be unstable and
   422	stakes-sensitive.

   424	\subsubsection{A decision-theoretic read-out}
   425	\label{sec:decision-readout}
   426	
   427	The threshold belongs in the read-out. A judge in $c$ chooses between
   428	\textsc{in-repertoire} and \textsc{not-in-repertoire} under asymmetric losses
   429	$L_{\mathrm{fa}}(c)$ and $L_{\mathrm{fr}}(c)$. The optimal rule accepts iff
   430	
   431	\begin{equation}
   432	g_{i,t}(u,c)\geq \tau(c)
   433	=\frac{L_{\mathrm{fa}}(c)}{L_{\mathrm{fa}}(c)+L_{\mathrm{fr}}(c)}.
   434	\end{equation}

   641	conjunct in~\eqref{eq:Gtheta} on which the token's status turns--after cue-based
   642	situation uncertainty has been integrated. Conditional on an independently fixed
   643	$c$, write $\nu_i(u,c)$; if the pivotal condition differs across
   644	high-probability conditioning states, the observable confidence read-out follows
   645	the modal condition and treats the remaining mixture as lower confidence. For
   646	structural and compatibility failures the pivotal condition is a fact about the
   647	assembly set, so once the relevant inventory is fixed that posterior is treated
   648	as effectively degenerate: confidence is high by modelling convention, not by a
   649	separate learning theorem. For licensing-pivotal tokens, $\nu_i$ is the Beta
   650	concentration of the pivotal node's posterior (\S\ref{sec:update}).
   651	
   652	Evidence confidence, the quantity that predicts framing lability, is
   653	\begin{equation}
   654	\Phi^{\mathrm{ev}}_{i,t}(u,e)=\frac{\nu_i(u,e)}{\nu_i(u,e)+\nu_0}
   655	\in [0,1),
   656	\end{equation}
   657	\noindent with $\nu_0$ a scaling constant. Confidence in a binary
   658	\textsc{in-repertoire}/\textsc{not-in-repertoire} verdict also depends on where
   659	the posterior mass lies relative to the decision threshold:
   660	\begin{equation}
   661	\Phi^{\mathrm{dec}}_{i,t}(u,e)=
   662	\max\!\left\{
   663	P\!\left(S_t^{\theta}(u,c^\ast)\ge\tau(c^\ast)\mid\mathcal{D}_{i,t},e\right),
   664	P\!\left(S_t^{\theta}(u,c^\ast)<\tau(c^\ast)\mid\mathcal{D}_{i,t},e\right)
   665	\right\}.
   666	\label{eq:phi-dec}
   667	\end{equation}

   669	$\Phi^{\mathrm{ev}}$ but low decision confidence if its mass lies near
   670	$\tau(c)$. Here $c^\ast$ is the modal conditioning state under $q_h(c\mid e)$;

   808	  $\mathcal{A}(f,v)=\emptyset$: no covering assembly in the fixed inventory.
   809	  $S_t^{\theta}=G_t=0$ up to inventory uncertainty; $\Phi\approx1$ under every
   810	  construal. Status: ungrammatical, structurally.
   811	
   812	\item \mention{Colorless green ideas sleep furiously}. A covering assembly is defined, saturated, and licensed node by node: $S_t^\theta\approx1$, with $G_t$ and a typical $g_{i,t}$ tracking it. The strangeness is a construal-plausibility cost in $R_i$: $F$ negative, status grammatical. Content-odd, not ill-formed.
   813	
   814	\item \ungram{\mention{I've finished it yesterday}} (compatibility failure). Covering assemblies exist, but every one sets the reference-interval dimension twice incompatibly--the perfect's contribution and the deictic adverb's don't unify--so $\operatorname{def}(A)=0$ throughout. $S_t^{\theta}=G_t=0$; $\Phi\approx1$. Entrenchment of the component constructions can't repair it: the clash is outside the licensing terms altogether.

   816	\item \ungram{\mention{I have 25 years}} (licensing failure). Defined and saturated; the value is transparent. The pivotal age-predication node has $\theta_t\approx0$, tracked by a typical speaker at $C^{(i)}_t\approx0$ with high $\nu^{(i)}$, after preemption by the \textsc{be}-frame in a dense niche. $g_{i,t}\approx0$ and $\Phi$ is high. Status: ungrammatical, by concentrated non-licensing.
   817	
   818	\item \marg{\mention{friend of whose}} (community-novel). Defined and saturated; the niche is vanishingly sparse, so a typical speaker's pivotal-node posterior sits near its prior with low $\nu^{(i)}$. The speaker's $g_{i,t}$ is intermediate, $\operatorname{Var}(S_t^{\theta}\mid\mathcal{D}_{i,t})$ is high, and $\Phi$ is low.    819	
   820	\item \ungram{\mention{Which did you buy car?}} (putative preempted gap).
   821	Defined and saturated; the dedicated discontinuity node--the only activated node

   823	fronting opportunities only in proportion to independently normed
   824	$\widetilde{\rho}_t^\star$. If that full-choice term is high, its posterior is
   825	driven to zero and concentrates there (\S\ref{sec:update}); if it is low, the
   826	case belongs closer to starvation. Status assignment requires the niche-norming
   827	protocol of \S\ref{sec:identification-c}.
