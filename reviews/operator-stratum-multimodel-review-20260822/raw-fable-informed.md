## 1. Strongest defensible thesis of the target paper

Grammaticality talk is selective, and the selection tracks a role, not a substance. A contrast belongs to the operator stratum when it is organized as a closed, recurrent paradigm in a community's repertoire and when that paradigm, taken as a whole over the occasions it is used, configures how utterances enter public update (what move is made, who is committed, what next moves are licensed). Because the licensing state of such a paradigm is concentrated by dense competition (the licensed value wins nearly every opportunity), exclusion attaches to paradigm membership rather than to the update value of the individual token: *goed* is out although its past-tense value is intact, and an inanimate concord error is out although that token disambiguates nothing. Clausal architecture is judged like tense and agreement exactly where it packages such paradigms (clause type, argument linking, dependency scope, reference tracking), and tonal, prosodic, gestural, or lexical material enters the stratum on the same terms. This yields testable signatures: form-targeted correction under the community's accountability profile, satiation resistance in dense paradigms, shared processing profiles across exponent substances, and no grammar-policing of update-relevant but open-class resources. The account claims a stable, projectible repertoire and a candidate stabilizer (preemption), not a homeostatic controller. Everything in the paper that is stronger than this (recruitment *because of* update value as a constitutive fact; "maintenance dynamics" as a mechanism; the "synchronic version is falsified" framing) is either a support hypothesis or a wording problem, as laid out below.

## 3. Ontic/epistemic and level-of-analysis audit

Levels in play: social-practical (accountability, repair, grammaticality talk); population/conventional (repertoire membership, "membership facts" T:38, "paradigm licensing" T:309); speaker-epistemic (expectation T:72, detection, felt ungrammaticality); processing/neural (T:159, 253, 335); diachronic-functional (recruitment, grammaticalization T:239); information-theoretic analogy (T:84, 243–253); normative (Millar, T:297).

Slips:

- **T:64** defines a world-side membership condition by a corpus estimator ("iff"), then calls the estimator imperfect.
- **T:169** makes an analyst-side property (projectibility of the profile) the cause of a speaker's feeling.
- **T:175** uses "projectible" to mean "constraining".
- **T:74, 155** "policing can attach to paradigm integrity", "Large opportunity sets can police wrong values": no bearer. Opportunity counts don't police; a population's evidence concentrates and speakers correct under accountability profiles. This is the mereological slip the house rules flag.
- **T:157 fn** moves from speaker-evidence level ("Bayesian") to population exclusion with no adoption step, the exact gap the companion's simulations were built to expose (M:219–221).
- **T:247–253** derives a processing prediction (globality) from the codebook analogy without naming the parsing mechanism (incremental reanalysis of material in scope). The analogy is flagged as analogy at T:84 but then does inferential work at T:253.
- **T:297** reports a signaling-game model as a demonstration about communities.
- **T:40** "maintenance dynamics make paradigm membership categorical": a dynamical claim placed where a constitutive one belongs.
- **T:21** abstract asserts the etiological "because" flat; the body hedges it (T:60 "hypothesis", T:207 "plausibly").

Ontic status of the target's "operator stratum": it is a population/conventional property (which contrasts the community has closed into update-configuring paradigms). Its estimate (closure, opportunity count, Δ by probe) and its predicted consequences (judgment, repair, ERP) are kept apart at T:161, but not at T:64 or T:169. One sentence fixes the pattern: membership is a fact about the repertoire; the diagnostics estimate it; judgments and repair are consequences.

## 5. Recruitment / maintenance / control audit

**Strongest world-side commitment actually supported:** there exist closed, recurrent contrast sets in community repertoires whose licensing states are concentrated (obligatory marking, categorical asterisks, paradigm closure are observational), whose paradigm-level contribution configures update (defensible for clause type, switch-reference, evidentials, tense, role marking; defensible for gender via cross-clausal reference tracking in the minority of cases, T:207–209), and whose mismatches attract form-targeted talk under accountability profiles. Plus substance-neutrality (Akan tone, ASL nonmanuals, particles). That is a stable profile, the companion's "profile" level, and nothing more.

**Claimed beyond that, for free:**

1. *Recruitment as cause.* T:21 "recruited because", T:40 "update-critical contrasts are the entry point". Hopper & Traugott and Bybee (T:239) support lexical → grammatical, not "because of update value". No diachronic evidence offered. Status: etiological hypothesis. The companion's obligatorification sketch (M:1368–1374) says what evidence would look like: a contrast obligatorifies niche by niche as its marked competitor wins.
2. *Stabilization as fact.* T:155, 157 fn, 253. The companion shows this requires an adoption response, not updating alone (M:1208–1219). Status: candidate stabilizer, conditional.
3. *Maintenance by naming.* T:40 "maintenance dynamics make", T:74 "maintenance condition", T:297 "what sustains it". The companion's maintenance grade needs removal or longitudinal evidence (M:1048–1054); none is cited. Status: the word is doing the work.
4. *Coordination convergence.* T:297 O'Connor "demonstrates". Signaling-game result.
5. *Control.* Disclaimed at T:349, but "policing" (T:23, 40, 74, 76, 207, 291) reads as corrective control to any linguist, and T:303 "Overt correction is rare in both cases" then undercuts it. The vocabulary implies what the conclusion denies.

One further point the target can use to its advantage: on the companion's own dynamics, Δ enters only the repair stream r(Δ, ι) (S3:291–293; M:1056–1072) and, by stipulation, the adoption criterion q_κ (M:1262–1265). The Beta update (M:919–923) and the inclusion transition (M:965–970) never see Δ, and equilibrium concentration scales with N/(1−δ_m) (M:1427). So a closed, dense contrast whose divergence has fallen keeps its concentration as long as the licensed allomorph keeps winning attributable opportunities. That is the gender case, and it is what the companion's filter predicts absent the q_κ stipulation. The target should say so: it relocates the "conflict" onto one stipulated line in the companion (M:1677 "gradient, unimodal licensing regardless of frequency"), which the companion's M:1592 already half-concedes.
