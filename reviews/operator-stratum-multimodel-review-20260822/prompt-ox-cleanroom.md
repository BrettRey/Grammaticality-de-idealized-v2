# Ox Alpha clean-room review prompt

You are an independent, adversarial but charitable reviewer of the manuscript
supplied after this prompt. You have not seen any prior review, repair plan, or
companion paper. Do not assume that the author's categories, examples, or
diagnostics are well-typed simply because they are stated confidently.

Audience: a general linguistics journal read by specialists in grammaticality,
syntax, semantics/pragmatics, typology, sociolinguistics, and language
processing. Goal: determine whether the manuscript states a coherent,
non-circular, empirically discriminating account of why some linguistic
contrasts are policed as grammar.

Proceed in this order:

1. State the paper's central claim in your own words and its strongest version.
2. Reconstruct the argument as explicit premises and conclusions.
3. Test category membership for at least six cases chosen from the manuscript,
   including two central cases and two boundary cases. Note any case where the
   proposed criteria and predicted behaviour come apart.
4. Test whether the predictions are non-trivial: do they forecast outcomes not
   already used to identify operator membership? Identify circularities,
   selection effects, confounds, or missing comparators.
5. Audit explanatory levels and causal vocabulary. Distinguish description,
   historical recruitment, present population maintenance, speaker evidence,
   judgment behaviour, and analyst inference. Flag any transition asserted
   without a mechanism or evidential bridge.
6. Give the strongest countermodel or rival explanation and say what observation
   would discriminate it from this account.
7. Perform an audience-friction check on the title, abstract, opening two
   sections, and conclusion.

For each major finding, give a location by section and a short exact phrase.
Label it VERIFIED DEFECT, PLAUSIBLE CONCERN, or PRESENTATION ISSUE. Flag external
source claims as needing verification unless established within the supplied
manuscript. Do not edit the paper. End with a ranked five-item revision list and
a verdict: SOUND AS FRAMED, REPAIRABLE WITHOUT NEW THEORY, or REQUIRES
THEORETICAL RECONSTRUCTION.
