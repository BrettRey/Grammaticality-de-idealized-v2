# Final Adjudication: Operator-Stratum Paper

## 1. Bottom-line verdict

**Sound core with reframing** (equivalently: repairable without new theory). All three reviews independently converge on the same diagnosis, and on re-reading the manuscript against their strongest objections, I confirm it. The defensible theory is: *grammaticality talk tracks a functional role, not a substance; policing attaches to the integrity of closed paradigms whose recruitment history runs through public-update roles; token-level update value is neither necessary nor sufficient synchronically; judgments are detector output, not the target.* What must be reframed, not patched: (a) the level of the public-update condition, (b) the status of *le hiver* and *depend of*, (c) the process-grade vocabulary ("maintenance," "policing"), and (d) honesty about which predictions discriminate against the entrenchment rival and which merely follow from closure plus frequency.

One genuine cross-paper incompatibility must be surfaced, not smoothed: the companion's M:1677 ("a closed, high-opportunity contrast that isn't update-configuring should be policed like style… gradient, unimodal licensing regardless of frequency") is (i) not derivable from the companion's own dynamics without the stipulated Δ-dependent adoption criterion q_κ, since Δ enters neither the Beta update nor the inclusion transition, and (ii) flatly contradicted by the gender case the companion itself defers to at M:1592. The target's resolution (paradigm-level condition (i), opportunity as modulator) is correct and the companion's prose should be brought to it — see §6 below.

## 3. Operator-stratum membership: surviving definition

**Technical definition.** A form–value relation belongs to the *operator stratum* of community c iff:

1. **(Closure)** it occupies a small, enumerable paradigm P in c's repertoire, whose values are recurrently selected in describable structural environments and mutually exclusive there; and
2. **(Paradigm-level update configuration)** P, taken over its opportunities of use, conventionally contributes to configuring public update — operationally, the expected divergence between the update/common-ground posterior with P's value set available versus ablated is reliably positive, measured by comprehension probes and, coarsely, by next-turn-type distributions restricted to update-type responses (answers, acknowledgements, refusals, challenges — excluding sanction-, stance-, and assessment-driven shifts).

**Opportunity density is not a membership condition.** It modulates the concentration of licensing expectations and hence the projections: categoricality, satiation resistance, repair rates, effective preemption.

**Token-level update contribution is neither necessary nor sufficient.** Individual tokens may be fully redundant (gender concord, *goed*) provided the paradigm-level contribution is positive.

**Exponent-level inheritance.** Individual exponents and allomorphs inside an operator paradigm (*went*, *l'*, a harmonic suffix, a concord marker) are not themselves operators; they inherit policing through paradigm integrity. This is what makes *le hiver* principled rather than ad hoc: definite reference is the operator value; *le/l'* are contextually licensed exponents in a closed determiner paradigm; \**le hiver* preserves the value and selects an unlicensed exponent — the same class as *goed* and Turkish disharmony, not an expression-shape case. The analogy to the companion's Turkish appendix is structurally sound. Two obligations attach: (i) cite the h-aspiré literature (the alternation is lexically listed — *le héros*, *le hibou* vs *l'homme* — which is exactly what the exponent analysis predicts); (ii) demote the case from "friendly boundary" to a stated test: if arbitrary allomorphy with no paradigm-level update role is policed as grammar, recruitment-by-containment wins and condition (2) must be demoted to diachrony across the board.

**Preserved falsifiers.**
- **F1 (against closure-only rivals):** matched closed paradigms, matched for opportunity count and competitor frequency, differing in paradigm-level update configuration, should differ in repair profile and processing signature.
- **F2 (against immunization):** a closed, dense paradigm with paradigm-level Δ ≈ 0 by probe whose mismatches nevertheless draw the value-error repair stream (not merely accountability-tracking correction) falsifies the membership definition. Historical recruitment stories may not be invented post hoc to absorb such a case.
- **F3 (upper bound):** a contrast failing closure is not an operator however policed. Policed non-operators exist (*depend of*, *less/fewer*, conjugation-class theme vowels) and are admitted as such — policed via valency/containment/prescriptive overlay, outside the stratum proper.

**Plain-language version.** A contrast is an operator when two things hold: speakers pick from a small, fixed menu of options at well-defined spots in the sentence, and the menu as a whole helps determine what the utterance is *doing* — asking rather than asserting, committing this participant rather than that one, keeping track of who did what — even if most individual uses of the menu are redundant. How often the choice comes up affects how sharp and fast the policing feels, not whether the contrast counts. And corrections are evidence about membership, not what membership is: a community can correct a form whose message came through perfectly, because what failed is the paradigm, not the update.

## 5. Discriminating predictions

**The three strongest, with rivals:**

1. **Cross-substance convergence.** In grammatical-tone languages, tonal TAMP mismatches pattern with segmental morphosyntactic anomalies (judgment, repair, ERP), while segmentally matched sociophonetic tone variation patterns as accent. *Rivals defeated:* substance-based accounts and the schooling-ideology account. *Rival that survives:* entrenchment — so the design must match tonal TAMP and sociophonetic tone for density and frequency; the discrimination lies in the matched contrast, not the raw effect.
2. **Repair-profile split in unstandardized communities.** Form-targeted repair of operator slots among adults where schooling cannot supply the metalinguistic category defeats the ideology rival; and the split between Δ-scaled repair (wrong values) and accountability-tracking correction (value-intact errors) — with the latter explicitly *not* claimed as the controller — answers the unfalsifiability objection to Prediction 1. Controls: recoverability of the target form, epistemic access, error class (Schegloff–Jefferson), L2/instructional confound.
3. **The paradigm/token dissociation, measured.** Comprehension-probe detection of paradigm-level update contribution (gender: probe-positive) combined with null token-level next-turn shift and persistent categorical correction — with F2 as the live falsifier. This is the only prediction that separates the account from pure closure-plus-frequency on synchronic data, because closure+frequency predicts policing but not *which* closed paradigms recruit, nor the probe dissociation.

**Present predictions that are merely consequences of closure/frequency or grammaticalization** (demote to consistency checks or corollaries): satiation resistance (entrenchment/preemption); categoricality scaling with opportunity count (entrenchment); obligatoriness → policing (near-tautological until OBL is derived); ERP N400/P600 recapitulation (known dissociation); "update-relevant open resources aren't policed" (follows from closure alone for most cases). The genuinely residual content of the public-update story is (a) the recruitment selector, now a diachronic conjecture with a stated test, and (b) the comparative-concept strata taxonomy. Claim novelty accordingly and modestly.

## 7. Canon recommendation

**Portfolio-wide canon — accept three entries:**

1. **World state vs evidence/estimate/read-out.** Membership facts are population/conventional properties; closure counts, probe divergences, and corpus codings are estimates; judgments, felt ungrammaticality, and repair behavior are speaker read-outs. No world-side category may be defined by an estimator ("iff… imperfect"), no population property may be attributed to an interaction ("opportunity sets police" — every process claim needs a bearer), and no read-out may be promoted to a membership criterion. *Justification:* this failure pattern recurs across the target (T:64, T:74, T:155, T:169, T:297) and motivated concrete repairs in the OVMG audit; it is a general discipline, not a local wording fix.
2. **Historical recruitment vs synchronic membership.** Etiological stories may explain how a contrast entered a regime; they may never serve as synchronic membership conditions or as post-hoc absorbers of counterexamples. A recruitment claim is admissible only with a stated diachronic test and a preserved falsifier. *Justification:* the immunization risk is structural, recurs whenever a theory has a historical arm, and the F2-style safeguard is reusable.
3. **Process grades: stabilization / maintenance / corrective control.** Naming a dynamical word does not create the mechanism; claims must be graded (stabilization by preemption = weakest, supported; maintenance loops = needs longitudinal evidence; corrective control = denied here and should be denied by default absent a demonstrated controller). *Justification:* the same grading resolved the OVMG repair and again did real work here.

**Not recommended for canon:** the specific operator-exponent distinction (valuable, but it is a linguistic analysis, not a methodological rule); the response-type taxonomy for next-turn coding (paper-family-specific instrument); the opportunity-as-modulator decision (correct, but it is a substantive theoretical commitment of this family, not a portable rule).

## 8. Simulation, formalization, Lean

**No.** New simulation, formalization, or Lean work would not materially increase evidential value at this stage.

- **Lean:** the target states no theorems; there is nothing to prove.
- **Simulation:** the relevant dynamical facts are already readable from the repaired companion's equations (Δ absent from the Beta update and inclusion transition; concentration scaling with N/(1−δ_m)); running new simulations would decorate a conceptual paper. The one simulation with residual value — whether Δ-dependent q_κ is needed at all — is a companion-side question and should be settled there before either paper's prose leans on M:1677.
- **Formalization:** exactly one import has evidential value: bringing the companion's Δ(d) in as a *paradigm-level expected divergence* converts the gender dissociation from a rhetorical contrast into a measurable quantity and restores falsifier F2. That is a definitional import, not new formal work.

The highest-yield next expenditure is empirical infrastructure, not modeling: the comprehension-probe design for paradigm-level Δ and the coded repair-profile corpus protocol (with the response-type taxonomy and the recoverability/access controls). Those, plus the revision plan above, are what move the paper's evidential weight.
