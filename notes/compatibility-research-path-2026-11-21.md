# From the miniature language back to grammaticality

I would give the next two working sessions to the English present perfect. The first would identify a contrast that matters for the manuscript. The second would give a serious rival the opportunity to predict it. The cabinet work already changes how I read the paper. Its claim that hard compatibility predicts categorical individual judgments requires assumptions about how speakers select an analysis and report a judgment. The claim worth pursuing is that a newly conventional construction has a temporal contribution we can discover independently. [Manuscript](../grammaticality-de-idealized.tex) · [Finite comparisons](../experiments/compatibility/README.md)

## 1. Find one English contrast worth predicting

Start with *I’ve finished it yesterday*. What does the proposed new analysis let a speaker convey, and what would a hearer understand on a fresh example? Select one variety and register. Compare contexts that make a current consequence salient with contexts organized around a past narrative. Define eligible occasions from the discourse with the verb form masked. Include the preterite choices. Reserve one family of adverbials for prediction before examining its distribution.

There is already a substantive rival. Werner compared perfect and preterite use across twelve components of the International Corpus of English. Bondar examines *yesterday*, *last* + noun phrase and *ago* in British and American corpora and argues that speakers can maintain present relevance while anchoring the event in the past. Both a constructional change and a discourse strategy could favour the same contexts. The first deliverable is a pair of contexts where precise versions of the accounts disagree, plus a paraphrase choice or discourse continuation that would expose the disagreement. [Werner (2013)](https://doi.org/10.1075/eww.34.2.04wer) · [Bondar (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1075/eww.25038.bon)

## 2. Give rival accounts the same prediction problem

Specify the OVMG reanalysis and an independently developed constructional learner on the same fragment. The learner must specify how a verb, marker and adverb combine, how alternative analyses compete and what transfers to a new combination. Give a categorical account with discourse inference or repair the same evidence. Use a simple predictor based on verb, adverb and discourse context as the benchmark. Freeze each account’s predictions for form choice and interpretation on the reserved items. Model theory does its work here by deriving a disagreement or showing why the accounts coincide.

- **A disagreement survives.** Generate observations under each account and fit every account to them. Allow plausible speaker differences, uncertain discourse coding and response error. Carry the contrast forward if the fitted models can distinguish it at feasible precision and can return an inconclusive answer where warranted.
- **The accounts coincide.** Try one observation with an independently motivated link to the difference, such as which reading survives under a change of discourse context. If equivalence persists, record what this case identifies and move on.

## 3. Let the available evidence choose the route

- **Existing English materials measure the contrast.** Fit on the development items and predict the reserved family. Corpus form choices can test a production prediction. A claim about what hearers recover also needs an interpretation measure.
- **English usage is documented but the decisive reading is unobserved.** Design the smallest paired production and interpretation study that could settle the disagreement. Pilot it only after the recovery result and a separate decision to proceed.
- **The tractable question is about learning from history.** Return to the miniature language for a controlled intervention. Hold positive exposures and their order fixed. Vary whether the competitor was chosen when the marker could have expressed the intended message. Test later choice and interpretation on an untrained combination. The existing order manipulation remains a separate test of recency. Its smallest predicted difference is 0.67 percentage points, and weighting at retrieval can reproduce weighting during learning. [Order-test derivation](../experiments/compatibility/README.md)

## 4. Decide how much theory the result earns

If one account predicts held-out production and interpretation better, carry its learning and response rules into a second construction. The manuscript’s dative alternation is a useful candidate because a speaker can prefer one expression when both are conventionally available. Predict the effect of a competitor on choice and what the speaker does when prompted to use the less preferred form. Add repair or confidence when accounts predict different responses. If the simpler benchmark travels equally well, narrow the proposed role for the extra machinery. If several accounts remain equivalent, report the recoverable generalization and revise the architectural claim accordingly.

The first paper-sized result would be a statement of which distinctions in conventional availability can be recovered from which observations, with one held-out human comparison and explicit counterexamples. The longer programme would ask whether the same account can predict adaptation, repair and confidence across constructions. Population dynamics can re-enter when such a result calls for a prediction about change through time.
