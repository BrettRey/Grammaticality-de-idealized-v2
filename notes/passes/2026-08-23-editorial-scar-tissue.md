## Deliberate retentions

Several contrastive or negative formulations remain because they have a named or
textually established opponent and perform genuine theoretical work. These
include the state/dynamics distinction, the operator/construction distinction,
hard compatibility versus re-licensing, preempted versus winnerless gaps, and the
maintenance/control evidential hierarchy. Removing those contrasts would erase
claims rather than revision history.

## Result

The scar tissue was real but localized. Most surviving seams came from good
adversarial corrections whose conversational framing had remained in the prose.
The edits remove that framing without relaxing the conditioning, warrant,
formal-scope, or falsification requirements.
