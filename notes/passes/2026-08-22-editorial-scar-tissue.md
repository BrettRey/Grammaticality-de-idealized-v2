## Result

The pass removed revision deixis and defensive assurances while preserving the
substantive distinctions forced by the formal review. In particular, it did not
weaken the separation between ontic state and epistemic estimate, the stated
limits of the simulations, or the evidential conditions on maintenance and
control.

After this whole-manuscript pass, the projectibility audit changed one sentence
about candidate support, and the house-style pass made only local connective,
contraction, citation-command, and page-break changes. A final spot-check found
no new review-facing or revision-deictic scar tissue in those edits.
