## Overall judgment

Projectibility is structural. It fixes the order of explanation in the
introduction, formal core, predictions, and conclusion: specify the profile,
state what partial observation licenses, and test possible supports separately.

The strongest result currently reached is model-level conditional
stabilization: the specified full map has separated attracting regions in some
parameter regimes, and the finite simulations supply existence and scope
checks. The manuscript doesn't establish that observed grammatical profiles
are maintained by the production--repair loop or controlled by repair. Those
remain empirical hypotheses with appropriate removal and intervention tests.

No structural reframing is now needed. The next evidential step is to fill in
study-specific populations, timescales, tolerances, and preregistered
comparators, then test longitudinal attribution and the repair intervention
signature.
