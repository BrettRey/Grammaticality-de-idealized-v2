# Reader pass: main paper

Date: 2026-08-23

Scope: `grammaticality-de-idealized.tex` and its included
`reader-model-and-dynamics.tex`, read in manuscript order. The formal supplement
was outside the prose-rewrite scope except where the main paper describes its
claims or limitations.

## Baseline diagnosis

The abstract was clear, but the body delayed the reader-facing model until after
several long diagnoses and then restated the same examples in the formalism,
change-mechanism, implications, and conclusion sections. A reader had to infer
the architecture from applications before receiving the architecture itself.
The legacy `Mechanisms of grammatical change` section also duplicated the newer
state/dynamics account.

Fable and Ox Alpha independently identified the same architectural problem. Ox
Alpha's initial word-count estimate was wrong, and its proposed ordering was
internally inconsistent; those parts were rejected. The shared recommendation
to move the complete reader-facing state and dynamics account forward was
accepted.

## Changes made

- Reordered the paper as introduction and short impasse, complete reader-facing
  state and dynamics theories, diagnostic applications, predictions,
  theoretical positioning, limitations, and conclusion.
- Removed the legacy change-mechanism section and consolidated repeated analyses
  of age predication, independent relative *whose*, left-branch extraction, and
  the dative alternation.
- Defined operator value and public update before they do diagnostic work.
- Preserved the state/dynamics split, population status versus speaker and
  analyst estimates, independently fixed conditioning, opportunity and the
  outside option, counterfactual choice, compatibility delimiters, the
  both-licensed dative control, and all conditionality and failure conditions.
- Shortened the theoretical comparisons and conclusion without removing their
  argumentative roles.
- Added reader-facing glosses for evidence concentration and accumulated
  counterfactually informative omissions.
- Corrected stale cross-references and removed formal vocabulary that had leaked
  into the math-free main paper.

## Regression review

Fable and Ox Alpha reread the revised files. Both judged the new order followable
and found every protected commitment intact. Their convergent residual findings
were accepted: define evidence concentration, remove undefined *preemption
mass*, state falsifiers for the framing-rank and L2-transfer predictions, repair
the moved dynamics pointer, and fix a conclusion sentence that confused the
conditions for informative testing with conditions for failure. Selected
positioning recaps were retained because they concede or delimit rival accounts
rather than re-diagnose the data.

## Result

The main paper fell from 47 to 31 pages and from about 17,900 to about 11,200
words. It still contains no inline or displayed mathematics. The final PDF was
read and visually inspected page by page; the model, tables, examples, figures,
section transitions, appendix, and references render legibly.
