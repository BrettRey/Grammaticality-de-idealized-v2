# Projectibility audit: main paper

Date: 2026-08-23

| Check | Status | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Declaration | GREEN | The introduction states the profile and projection before support (`grammaticality-de-idealized.tex:278–287`); the reader model fixes the target population and situation and states held-out repair, exposure, transmission, and change targets (`reader-model-and-dynamics.tex:25–41, 167–190`). |
| Inquiry/use, warrant plan, revision rule | GREEN | The paper requires independently fixed situations and niches, converging non-rating evidence, held-out targets, and narrowing, splitting, or abandoning a failed projection (`reader-model-and-dynamics.tex:184–190, 275–303`). |
| Non-trivial projection | GREEN | Repair, framing and exposure response, transmission, moribundity, and trajectories of change aren't membership diagnostics (`reader-model-and-dynamics.tex:173–185`; `grammaticality-de-idealized.tex:624–785`). |
| Warrant vs world-side | GREEN | Evidence concentration, population state, and analyst estimate are distinct, while simulations are described as conditional coherence checks rather than empirical warrant (`reader-model-and-dynamics.tex:136–162, 405–422`). |
| World-side order (no free ladder) | GREEN | Stability, stabilization, maintenance, and control are named as different claims; evidence for one doesn't establish the next (`reader-model-and-dynamics.tex:193–199`). |
| Stabilizer vs controller | GREEN | Repair remains a candidate maintainer or controller. Maintenance requires removal/transmission evidence; control requires deviation registration, a lower-level response, and restoration of the threatened relation (`reader-model-and-dynamics.tex:424–446`). |
| Level and mereology | GREEN | The paper distinguishes population licensing, a speaker's evidence and response, and an analyst's sample; adoption and retention, not belief precision, change the population (`reader-model-and-dynamics.tex:136–162, 375–390`). |
| Scope and field-relativity | GREEN | Community, variety, register, and norm centre are fixed independently; cross-linguistic comparisons require comparable niches, and transport can fail under population shift (`reader-model-and-dynamics.tex:25–41`; `grammaticality-de-idealized.tex:508–514, 924–929`). |
| Prospective revision | GREEN | The general revision rule and local falsifiers are explicit, including framing-rank reversal, L2 frequency defeating L1 history, matched non-operator repairs, and matched loss/recovery retracing one boundary (`reader-model-and-dynamics.tex:184–190`; `grammaticality-de-idealized.tex:652–654, 681–682, 724–727, 760–763`). |
| Positioning and conclusion | GREEN | The conclusion cashes out the category in projections and states the support claims at their actual strength (`grammaticality-de-idealized.tex:1094–1128`). |

Projectibility is structural: it determines the model's empirical payoff and the
order of explanation. The strongest model-internal result is conditional
stabilization under specified adoption and retention conditions. The manuscript
doesn't claim that its illustrative data establishes an actual maintainer or
controller; those remain hypotheses with separate intervention signatures.
